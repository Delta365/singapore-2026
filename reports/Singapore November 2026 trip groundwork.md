# Timing fixes tighten an eight-day Singapore plan

The agreed eight-day plan suits a couple based at Hillview who want two or three anchors a day: the Downtown Line runs from their station to the Botanic Gardens, Bugis, Bayfront, Telok Ayer, Chinatown and Fort Canning without a change, and each day's stops sit in two or three walkable clusters. Checked against the research notes, nine items in the plan need correcting, and most of them are opening hours. Ah Tai at Maxwell is **closed on Tuesdays**, the day it is scheduled; Golden Mile's char siu stall closes by 2pm while Sunday's order reaches it late in the day; the Zoo route card names a bus that does not run from Khatib; and the only LANY tickets on offer are resale seats the venue says it will not honour. Several items from the travellers' own list are missing and fit existing days without strain, among them the three Chinatown bakeries, Birds of Paradise gelato, the Tarik saffron tea shop and a walking tour. The largest outside risk is smoke haze: central Singapore's 24-hour PSI reached 181 on 9 October, and the Meteorological Service expects strong El Niño conditions to peak between October and December, which points to a hotter and drier November than the long-run average ([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/amp/news/singapore/2026/10/09/singapore-central-region-hits-2026-record-of-181-psi-amid-transboundary-haze/238371); [MSS](https://www.weather.gov.sg/climate-el-la/)). Deepavali on Sunday 8 November turns the last three days into a public-holiday weekend, so attractions will be busier, some family stalls may close, and the first train from Hillview on Monday 9 November is not until **05:54**, which makes the planned 04:30 taxi to Terminal 3 the right choice. Spending on the plan as agreed comes to about **S$1,560 (₹1.15 lakh) for two before accommodation**, more than half of it on food, with the Keng Eng Kee chilli crab the largest single meal.

## The agreed days hold together, with nine clashes to correct

Monday 2 to Friday 6 November are ordinary working days in the school term, and Saturday 7 to Monday 9 November form one of 2026's six long weekends, with Deepavali on the Sunday and the public holiday in lieu on the Monday ([MOM](https://www.mom.gov.sg/employment-practices/public-holidays); [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/singapore/2025/06/16/mark-your-calendars-singaporeans-get-six-long-weekends-in-2026-for-travel-sleep-ins-or-just-escaping-the-boss/180547)). The notes infer that Tuesday to Thursday will be the quietest days of the trip and Saturday to Monday the busiest at malls, the Mandai parks and Gardens by the Bay. The agreed plan follows that pattern. The Orchid Garden, Sungei Buloh, the Zoo and the East Coast bikes fall on weekdays, and the weekend goes to Telok Ayer, the bay and Kampong Glam. The stop lists look long, yet each day's stops cluster around two or three anchors within walking distance of one another. Thursday and Saturday are the heaviest days: Thursday runs from Sungei Buloh at 7am to dinner on Orchard Road, and Saturday strings eight stops from Ya Kun to Garden Rhapsody, so on either day the Orchard evening or the time in the National Gallery can shrink without losing the day's purpose.

### The iPhone purchase on Tuesday

The agreed plan now puts an iPhone purchase at Apple Marina Bay Sands on Tuesday afternoon, between Chinatown Complex and the Esplanade, with Apple Orchard Road as the backup on Thursday evening. The dedicated purchase note had not reached the research folder when this report was written, so store hours, prices and model availability are not covered here. The refund mechanics are documented. Under the Tourist Refund Scheme, goods packed in checked luggage are validated at the eTRS kiosk in the check-in hall before bag drop, and goods carried by hand, which will normally include a new phone, are validated at the kiosk in the transit area after immigration; approved goods must leave Singapore within 12 hours ([Singapore Customs](https://www.customs.gov.sg/at-customs/departing-singapore/tourist-refund-scheme/)). Changi's refund counters run 24 hours, and at Terminal 3 they sit between check-in rows 5 and 6 and in the transit area ([Changi Airport](https://www.changiairport.com/en/airport-guide/departing/immigration-and-customs.html)). The S$100 minimum spend per receipt is widely reported but was not verified in the notes. With counters closing 40 minutes before departure on Monday, the refund claim belongs after immigration, once bags are checked.

### How each day's eating works

Tuesday holds 13 of the stalls in the table, and every one of them except Ah Tai is open that day. The plan's order works: chicken rice when Tiong Bahru's stall opens at 10am, Maxwell for rojak, oyster cake and Old Nyonya's laksa before the noon rush, J2 at Amoy Street (about 300 m away) before it closes at 3pm, and Chinatown Complex in the afternoon, finishing before 168 CMY Satay closes at 7pm. At Lau Pa Sat, Boon Tat Street closes to traffic and about ten satay hawkers set up from around 7pm. The longest queues are at stalls 7 and 8, Best Satay, whose Set A of 26 sticks cost S$31.20 in May 2026, and one 2026 visitor rated the satay average while praising the setting ([Seth Lui](https://sethlui.com/lau-pa-sat-food-guide-singapore/); [Not Quite Nigella](https://www.notquitenigella.com/2026/07/25/lau-pa-sat/)). Bak kwa is the one purchase with a border question: India requires a Sanitary Import Permit for commercial pork imports, and the notes found no official passenger allowance, so sealed packs declared at Mumbai are the cautious choice ([ChemLinked](https://food.chemlinked.com/foodpedia/india-meat-regulation)).

Wednesday's crab dinner is the trip's biggest food decision. Time Out quotes Keng Eng Kee's chilli crab at about S$100 a kilo and advises booking because crab often sells out for walk-ins ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/singapore/restaurants/keng-eng-kee-seafood)). In 2023 police were called over a S$938 chilli crab bill after a diner misread a per-100 g price, so the couple should confirm the unit and the weight before the crab is cooked ([The Drinks Business](https://thedrinksbusiness.com/2023/09/police-called-over-s938-chilli-crab-charge)). The NUS lunch is a cheap, ordinary meal. Frontier is about 590 m from Kent Ridge MRT, and early November falls in the last teaching weeks before the 14 November reading week, so the canteen is crowded between about 11:45am and 1:30pm ([Eatbook](https://eatbook.sg/nus-food/); [NUS Registrar](https://nus.edu.sg/registrar/docs/default-source/calendar/ay2026-2027.pdf)). Public access and payment methods were not confirmed, which is why the plan's advice to carry a card and cash is sound.

Thursday evening on Orchard Road is the easiest food evening of the week, since everything is indoors. The Takashimaya Food Hall on Basement 2 was listed at 10am to 9:30pm in January 2024 and has a Japanese food street including Ebisu Sando's S$28 wagyu sandwich ([Mothership](https://mothership.sg/2024/01/japanese-food-street-takashimaya//amp)). Sun with Moon has served kamameshi at Wheelock Place since 2005 and dinner tables are hard to get, so booking matters more than the unverified closing time ([Burpple](https://www.burpple.com/f/kbv6rV63); [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/singapore/restaurants-and-cafes/sun-with-moon)). Thursday is also one of Standing Sushi Bar's S$3.90 salmon nights, a cheap alternative if Sun with Moon is full.

Friday covers the east. Keng Wah Sung has been family-run for over 70 years and serves kopi, charcoal kaya toast and handmade sugar doughnuts ([Eatbook](https://eatbook.sg/keng-wah-sung-doughnuts/)). 328 Katong Laksa and Janggut Laksa share family roots and dispute the "original" title; one guide says most people cannot tell them apart blind ([HungryGoWhere](https://hungrygowhere.com/critics-reviews/328-katong-laksa-vs-janggut-laksa/)). At Old Airport Road, "Oats" in Aunty Oats Pancake refers to the owner rather than an oat ingredient (an inference from the stall's naming), and Eatbook recommends the coconut slice and the kaya folded pancake ([Eatbook](https://eatbook.sg/aunty-oats-pancake/)). Nam Sing Hokkien Mee at #01-32 is a 2026 Bib Gourmand but closes on random days and sells out ([Eatbook](https://eatbook.sg/old-airport-road-food/)).

Sunday is the riskiest food day, because many hawkers rest on Sundays and Deepavali adds a holiday. ## Smoke haze is the likeliest disruption, and Deepavali reshapes the final weekend

### November's averages say wet, and 2026's signals say hot and dry

On the 1991 to 2020 averages, November is one of Singapore's wetter months: 252.3 mm of rain over about 19 rain days, a mean daily maximum of 31.2 °C and minimum of 24.6 °C, humidity near 85 percent, and 22 lightning days, the most of any month ([MSS](https://www.weather.gov.sg/climate-climate-of-singapore/)). Early November is the second inter-monsoon period, with light winds and thunderstorms in the afternoon and early evening; the Northeast Monsoon does not start until December. This year leans the other way. The Meteorological Service classified conditions as El Niño on 16 October, with the index at 2.1 °C in September and forecast to peak from October to December, and a positive Indian Ocean Dipole expected to last through November; both push towards drier and hotter weather ([MSS](https://www.weather.gov.sg/climate-el-la/)). Its outlook for early October forecast highs of 34 °C to 35 °C on most days ([New Straits Times](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/world/region/2026/10/1545944/singapore-face-hot-weather-thunderstorms-early-october)).

The Open-Meteo forecast fetched on 25 October for the trip map shows daily highs of about 30 °C to 31 °C, feels-like temperatures of 34 °C to 37 °C, a UV index of 8 to 9, and rain chances peaking between about 5pm and 7pm on most days. Its ten-year average for 2 to 9 November is about 30 °C by day and 24 °C at night, with rain recorded on each date in 8 to 10 of the last 10 years ([Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/)). A forecast 8 to 15 days ahead carries low confidence. The Meteorological Service's fortnightly outlook for 1 to 15 November should appear around 31 October and is the better guide. Both sources agree on the shape of a day: bright, hot mornings for outdoor sights and a storm risk in the late afternoon. The plan already rests at midday, keeps Thursday evening indoors and puts Saturday's late afternoon in the domes, and its own weather notes advise getting off the East Coast bikes by about 5:30pm. Lightning is a bigger operational risk than rain, since outdoor facilities in Singapore commonly suspend use during lightning alerts; that is general practice, not confirmed for named venues in the notes.

### Haze pushed PSI past 180 three weeks before arrival

Transboundary smoke from fires in Sumatra and Kalimantan put Singapore's air in the unhealthy range repeatedly from September ([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/singapore/2026/09/10/singapore-haze-pushes-air-quality-into-unhealthy-range-again/234637)). The central region's 24-hour PSI reached 181 at noon on 9 October and 191 by 4pm, and a 16 October report cited readings of 225 in the centre without stating when they were taken ([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/amp/news/singapore/2026/10/09/singapore-central-region-hits-2026-record-of-181-psi-amid-transboundary-haze/238371); [MustShareNews](https://mustsharenews.com/?p=771859); [New Straits Times](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/world/region/2026/10/1557122/singapore-issue-notices-seven-indonesian-firms-over-haze)). Around 15 October NEA forecast more showers and a shift to southerly winds that could carry less smoke from Kalimantan, but the notes hold no reading after 16 October ([Human Resources Online](https://www.humanresourcesonline.net/another-reason-to-look-forward-to-the-weekend-showers-forecast-over-hazy-singapore)). SIA closes check-in 40 minutes before departure, which is 40 minutes before departure for the outbound flight, and recommends arriving three hours early ([Singapore Airlines](https://www.singaporeair.com/pt_BR/travel-info/check-in/counter-checkin/)). Singapore Food Street on Level 3 of the Terminal 3 transit area opens at 6am, and Ya Kun in the public Basement 2 runs 24 hours ([Changi Airport](https://nowboarding.changiairport.com/discover-changi/affordable-authentic-food-court-fare-at-changi-airport.html); [Changi Airport](https://nowboarding.changiairport.com/discover-changi/changi-airport-terminal-3-food-guide.html)). Changi will be busy with residents returning from the long weekend that Monday.

## The arrival card opens on 31 October, and new bus rules fine even a sip of water

### Entry and customs

With the visas in hand, the remaining entry step is the SG Arrival Card, which each traveller must submit free of charge within the three days before arrival through ICA's e-Service or the MyICA app ([Changi Airport](https://changiairport.com/en/airport-guide/arriving/immigration-and-customs.html)). For a 2 November arrival that window opens on Saturday 31 October, and the e-Pass arrives at the email address given on the card, so each should use an inbox they can read on their phone ([MustShareNews](https://mustsharenews.com/ica-automated-lanes/)). First-time visitors with ICAO-compliant passports can use the automated lanes at Changi without enrolment. Sites that charge a fee to file the card are unofficial. The six-month passport validity rule is widely reported but was not confirmed on ICA's page in the notes.

Customs rules have tightened in 2026. Vaping now carries fines of up to S$10,000 under the Tobacco and Vaporisers Control Act in force since 1 May 2026, and ICA caught 56 travellers with vapes over four days in July, about 77 percent of them short-term visitors ([Tobacco Asia](https://www.tobaccoasia.com/news/singapore-escalates-vaping-crackdown/); [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/07/25/56-travellers-caught-entering-singapore-with-vapes-over-four-days-more-than-444-items-seized)). There has been no duty-free tobacco allowance since January 2023, the alcohol concession applies only after 48 hours away and sources disagree on the quantities, and cash over S$20,000 must be declared ([EntryBrief](https://entrybrief.com/customs/singapore/); [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/malaysia/2024/07/03/singapores-new-s20000-cash-declaration-rules-what-malaysians-need-to-know/142583)). Prescription medicines should travel in their original packaging with a doctor's letter; the notes did not check which Indian prescriptions, such as codeine cough syrups, need approval from the Health Sciences Authority, so that is worth a look on its site before packing.

### Safety and emergencies

The 2026 police advisories in the notes concern phishing rather than street crime: fake LTA toll messages, fake police pop-ups and fake Singtel texts. Genuine government SMS come only from the "gov.sg" sender ID, and the ScamShield helpline is 1799 ([Singapore Police Force](https://www.police.gov.sg/Media-Hub/News/2026/02/20260210_police_advisory_on_phishing_scams_targeting_singaporean_travellers_to_malaysia); [Open Government Products](https://www.open.gov.sg/products/scamshield)). The emergency numbers are 999 for police and 995 for ambulance and fire, with 1777 for a non-emergency ambulance ([Singapore Police Force](https://www.police.gov.sg/contact-us); [Expatica](https://expatica.com/sg/health/healthcare/singapore-emergency-number-2172794)). The High Commission of India is at 31 Grange Road, and secondary sources give its emergency line as +65 9172 9803 ([Sarkaritel](https://www.sarkaritel.com/?p=415619)). The nearest major emergency department to Hillview is Ng Teng Fong General Hospital in Jurong East, which publishes live waiting times ([NTFGH](https://www.ntfgh.com.sg/I-Want-to/Pages/Contact-NTFGH.aspx)). Singapore's tourist SIMs undercut Indian roaming: Changi lists a Singtel eSIM at S$12 for 100 GB over 14 days ([Changi Airport](https://www.changiairport.com/id/en/at-changi/facilities-and-services-directory/changi-airport-sim-card-purchase-buy-sim-card-singapore.html)). The Indian SIM should stay active for bank OTPs.

### Etiquette that now carries fines

Since 23 September 2026, eating or drinking on a public bus, including plain water, carries a fine of up to S$500, matching the long-standing rule on trains, and soiling a bus or interchange can cost up to S$5,000 ([TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/bus-rules-singapore/)). The rule is six weeks old and most guidebooks predate it, so the simple habit is to drink before boarding anything. At hawker centres, leaving trays, tissues or cans on the table has counted as littering since September 2021, with warnings first and fines for repeat offenders ([MSE](https://www.mse.gov.sg/latest-news/written-reply-to-pq-on-fines-for-not-returning-trays/)). Many centres have separate return racks for halal and non-halal crockery, and a tissue packet on a seat means it is taken. Temples require covered shoulders and knees, and shoes come off at Hindu temples and mosques. The Sultan Mosque admits visitors from 10am to noon and 2pm to 4pm except on Fridays, closes for prayers from 12:30pm to 2pm, lends robes at the counter, and does not admit visitors to the prayer hall unless a mosque official accompanies them ([Holidify](https://www.holidify.com/places/singapore/sultan-mosque-singapore-sightseeing-121125.html)). At the kampong, the residents' privacy is the rule. Ordering kopi follows a pattern of base drink, milk type, then modifiers: kopi-c is coffee with evaporated milk, kopi-o is black with sugar, kosong means no sugar, peng means iced, and da bao means takeaway ([Expat Living](https://expatliving.sg/coffee-fans-how-to-order-kopi-at-a-hawker-centre/)).

## Conclusion

Because Hillview's line reaches nearly every anchor without a change, travel time is rarely what limits this plan. The clashes are almost all about the clock: a stall's rest day, a closing time, a trail notice or a ticket that cannot be bought. The remaining work is therefore small and concrete: a handful of calls or checks in the week before (Keng Eng Kee and Sun with Moon bookings, Heap Seng Leong and Geylang Claypot Rice on Deepavali, Aunty Oats' hours, The Intan's appointment, the NParks notice for the Forest Walk) and the nine corrections to the plan's cards. The iPhone adds one more check, the refund kiosk after immigration, and its research note should confirm prices and stock when it arrives.

The haze risk is also more manageable than the October readings suggest, because Wednesday, Thursday and Friday can swap places. Each depends only on being a weekday: the NUS canteen, Lina, bus 925, Keng Wah Sung and the regular-day Zoo price all hold from Wednesday to Friday, while Tuesday is tied to its free Esplanade concert and Saturday to stalls and shows already fixed to that date. Haze episodes this season have also been short: central PSI of 123 on 4 September had eased to moderate by 6 September ([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/singapore/2026/09/06/singapore-haze-eases-after-unhealthy-readings-but-smoke-threat-lingers/234077)). If one morning's reading is high, pushing that day's outdoor plan back by a day or two is a realistic response. That option survives only if the Zoo tickets, the Keng Eng Kee table and The Intan appointment can move, so it is worth arranging them by phone or on changeable terms.
