# Singapore sites, heritage architecture and shopping, 2 to 9 November 2026 (couple based at Hillview MRT, DT3)

Research date: 24 to 25 October 2026. All prices in Singapore dollars (S$) as published on the date of research unless stated. "Tourist" means the non-resident or standard rate. Prices for two adults are simple doubles of the adult rate.

Coordinate method: all coordinates below come from the Singapore Land Authority OneMap search API (`https://www.onemap.gov.sg/api/common/elastic/search?searchVal=...`), queried on 25 October 2026, unless marked otherwise. OneMap returns the government's own building and street points. For a street or park, OneMap returns a single representative point (often a centroid), so a pin will land somewhere along the street or inside the park, not on a specific gate. Where OneMap returned nothing, the coordinate is marked "no reliable coordinate" and is not guessed. Source shorthand in this file: [OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/).

Important calendar facts for the whole trip:
- Deepavali 2026 falls on Sunday 8 November, and Monday 9 November is the in-lieu public holiday ([Office Holidays](https://www.officeholidays.com/countries/singapore/deepavali.php); [Budget Direct 2026 holiday guide](https://www.budgetdirect.com.sg/travel-insurance/articles/Singapore-Public-Holidays-Long-Weekend-Guide-2026)). So Sat 7, Sun 8 and Mon 9 November form a long weekend, and attractions will be busier and charge peak or weekend rates on those days.
- Monday 2 November is an ordinary Monday, when several museums close (see the closures section).

## 1. Gardens by the Bay: components, 2026 prices, light show and maintenance closures

### Takeaway
The outdoor gardens and Supertree Grove are free and open 5am to 2am; Garden Rhapsody is free at 7.45pm and 8.45pm nightly, and the November 2026 edition is "Garden Waltz" (1 to 26 Nov). Tourists cannot buy a Flower Dome-only ticket: the cheapest dome entry is the S$46 Flower Dome plus Cloud Forest combination (S$92 for two), and that is the part most worth paying for. Floral Fantasy closes 3 Nov, OCBC Skyway 4 Nov and Supertree Observatory 5 Nov for maintenance; the two domes have no closures between 2 and 9 November.

## 2. The Merlion statues and crowd timing

### Takeaway
The original Merlion is at Merlion Park, One Fullerton (1.28681, 103.85453). A second official Merlion stands on Mount Faber. Other statues exist but I could not geocode all of them reliably.

### Cited Findings
- Merlion Park: 21 Esplanade Drive, One Fullerton, 1.28681, 103.85453 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)).
- Merlion (Mount Faber), Mount Faber Road: 1.27117, 103.81909 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)). This sits near the start of the Southern Ridges walk (section 16).
- Tourism Court, 1 Orchard Spring Lane (Singapore Tourism Board building): 1.30360, 103.82407 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)). OneMap returned the building, not a statue.
- Nearest MRT for Merlion Park: Raffles Place (EW14/NS26), Exit A at 1.28413, 103.85144 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)), about 400 to 500 m away by straight-line distance.

### Inferences
- From general knowledge, not verified in this session: the large Merlion at Merlion Park has a small "Merlion cub" statue behind it; a further Merlion stands at the Tourism Court; two small Merlions stand at Ang Mo Kio Avenue 1; the large Sentosa Merlion was demolished in 2019. These should be confirmed before plotting.
- For photographs, early morning (around 7 to 8am) is quietest and gives soft light with the Marina Bay Sands skyline behind. Evenings from 7pm onwards are the busiest, though the lit skyline is the main draw.

### Gaps
- I did not find an authoritative list of all Merlion statues, nor OneMap coordinates for the Merlion cub, Tourism Court statue or Ang Mo Kio statues. No reliable coordinate for those.

## 3. Chinatown district walk

### Takeaway
All the Chinatown sights sit within about 1 km, between Chinatown MRT and Telok Ayer MRT, both on the Downtown Line direct from Hillview. Temples require covered shoulders and knees. A sensible order runs north to south then east: Chinatown Heritage Centre, Masjid Jamae, Sri Mariamman, Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, Ann Siang Hill and Club Street, Telok Ayer Street and Thian Hock Keng.

### Cited Findings
- Buddha Tooth Relic Temple and Museum: 288 South Bridge Road, 058840; 1.28149, 103.84424 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)). Official hours 9am to 5pm daily per the temple's own contact page; other guides give 7am to 5pm or 7am to 7pm. Free; donations welcome. Shoulders and knees must be covered; shorts, mini-skirts and off-shoulder tops are not allowed; sarongs available at the entrance. No photography on the fourth floor relic chamber. ([Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, contact](https://www.buddhatoothrelictemple.org.sg/lianxin/contact); [Singapore Spirit guide](https://www.singapore-spirit.com/guides/buddha-tooth-relic-temple/))
- Sri Mariamman Temple: 244 South Bridge Road, 058793; 1.28268, 103.84516 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)).
- Masjid Jamae (Chulia): 218 South Bridge Road, 058767; 1.28319, 103.84535 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)).
- Thian Hock Keng: 158 Telok Ayer Street, 068613; 1.28108, 103.84751 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)). One guide lists hours as daily 7.30am to 5pm ([HoneyKids Asia](https://honeykidsasia.com/best-temples-in-singapore-to-visit-with-kids/)).
- Chinatown Heritage Centre: 48 Pagoda Street, 059207; 1.28358, 103.84438 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)). Official site lists daily 10am to 7pm, last entry 6pm; an official promotions page lists 10am to 8pm, last entry 7.15pm. Third-party listings give S$25 adult non-resident (S$15 resident). ([Chinatown Heritage Centre](https://www.chinatownheritagecentre.com.sg/); [Pelago](https://www.pelago.com/en-SG/activity/pt0lpejiy-chinatown-heritage-centre-singapore/))
- Ann Siang Hill Park: 1.28079, 103.84628; Club Street: 1.28241, 103.84621; Telok Ayer Street: 1.28108, 103.84782 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)).
- MRT: Chinatown (DT19) 1.28422, 103.84514; Telok Ayer (DT18), Exit A 1.28189, 103.84851; Maxwell (TE18), Exit 1 1.28104, 103.84416 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)).

### Inferences
- Walking order: start at Chinatown MRT (DT19), Pagoda Street exit, for the Heritage Centre and the Pagoda Street shophouses; walk to South Bridge Road for Masjid Jamae and Sri Mariamman, which stand side by side; continue south to Buddha Tooth Relic Temple; lunch at Maxwell Food Centre opposite; climb Ann Siang Hill to Ann Siang Road (Kokuyo, section 13) and Club Street; descend to Telok Ayer Street for Thian Hock Keng; finish at Telok Ayer MRT (DT18). Allow three to four hours with lunch.
- Go in the morning, when temples are active and it is cooler. Club Street and Ann Siang Road are quiet by day and liveliest after 6pm, especially Friday and Saturday.
- Sri Mariamman is Singapore's oldest Hindu temple, so it will be very busy on Deepavali, Sunday 8 November. That may be a reason to visit or to avoid that day, depending on their tolerance for crowds.

### Gaps
- No official hours or dress rules were found for Sri Mariamman Temple, Masjid Jamae or Thian Hock Keng. Modest dress (covered shoulders and knees, shoes off in the Hindu temple and mosque) is standard, but I did not verify each temple's rule.
- The official 2026 Chinatown Heritage Centre price chart is an image that I could not read; the S$25 figure is from a reseller.

## 4. Kampong Glam and Arab Street

### Takeaway
The Malay Heritage Centre reopened on 25 April 2026 after a revamp and is open Tuesday to Sunday, 10am to 6pm, closed Mondays. Sultan Mosque admits visitors in limited morning and afternoon windows, with a reduced window on Fridays.

### Cited Findings
- Masjid Sultan (Sultan Mosque): 3 Muscat Street, 198833; 1.30224, 103.85900 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)). Visitor hours commonly listed as Saturday to Thursday 10am to 12pm and 2pm to 4pm, Friday 2.30pm to 4pm; sources conflict, and one lists Friday as closed. Modest dress and shoe removal required; free. ([Holidify](https://www.holidify.com/places/singapore/sultan-mosque-singapore-sightseeing-121125.html); [Travjoy](https://travjoy.com/singapore/sultan-mosque-masjid-sultan-8hsiv4); [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/--d317468.html))

Reopened 25 April 2026 with a new narrative "Dari Rantau Ke Rumah" (From the Region to Home). Free for all visitors 25 April to 28 June 2026; after that, free for Singaporeans and PRs. Tuesday to Sunday, 10am to 6pm (last admission 5.30pm), closed Mondays. ([NHB media release](https://www.nhb.gov.sg/-/media/nhb/files/media/releases/2026/media-release---mhc-reopening.pdf); [Malay Heritage Centre](https://malayheritage.gov.sg/); [PMO](https://www.pmo.gov.sg/newsroom/pm-lawrence-wong-at-the-malay-heritage-centres-reopening-festival-apr-2026/))

- Nearest MRT: Bugis (EW12/DT14). DT14 platform point 1.29931, 103.85713 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)). Direct from Hillview on the Downtown Line.

### Inferences
- Best photographs: Bussorah Street with the mosque's golden dome at its end, in the morning when the sun is behind the camera and the street is quiet. Haji Lane murals photograph best before about 11am, before shops open and crowds arrive. The area is liveliest in the evening, when the Arab Street and Bussorah Street restaurants fill.
- Textile and rug shops line Arab Street and the adjoining blocks; no individual shop was verified in this session.
- Do not visit on Monday 2 November if the Malay Heritage Centre matters to them.

### Gaps

## 8. Heritage residential architecture: bungalows, terraces, house museums

### Takeaway
Two of the four house museums are closed: NUS Baba House is closed for restoration until 2027, and Sun Yat Sen Nanyang Memorial Hall has been closed since 16 September 2024, also until 2027. A Baba House slot on Tuesday 3 November is therefore not possible. The Intan (by appointment) and the Eurasian Heritage Gallery (S$5, walk-in, closed Mondays) remain open. The three worth the trip: (1) Joo Chiat and Koon Seng Road Peranakan terraces combined with a booked visit to The Intan; (2) Tiong Bahru's 1930s flats or Blair Road and Emerald Hill for shophouse typology; (3) Dempsey Hill's black and white bungalows, combined with the Botanic Gardens.

### Cited Findings
- NUS Baba House, 157 Neil Road: temporarily closed for extensive restoration and conservation works and "will reopen in 2027"; a 2025 "Baba House Exposed" architectural tour ran Thursday to Saturday at 10am from 10 July 2025; a "Blair Plain Walking Tour" was announced with details "to be announced"; contact babahouse@nus.edu.sg ([NUS Baba House, Plan your visit](https://babahouse.nus.edu.sg/plan-your-visit/); [NUS Giving, Oct 2026](https://nus.edu.sg/nusgiving/news-article/2026/10/01/keeping-heritage-alive-at-nus-baba-house)). Before closure, tours were capped at 13 people and booking was essential ([Baba House heritage tours](https://babahouse.nus.edu.sg/programmes/heritage-tours)). Coordinate 1.27721, 103.83735 ([OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)).

### Inferences
- Kampong Buangkok from Buangkok MRT: the kampong is about 1.7 km west of the station in a straight line, so roughly a 25-minute walk; a short taxi is simpler. Allow 30 to 45 minutes on site. Go early on Sunday morning, keep voices low, stay on the shared lane, ask before photographing any person, and do not enter yards. It is a small, quiet place; the value is the contrast with the surrounding HDB estate, not sights.
- From Hillview to Buangkok: Downtown Line to Little India (DT12), then North East Line to Buangkok (NE15); about 50 minutes.
- From Hillview to Tiong Bahru: Downtown Line to Stevens (DT10), then Thomson-East Coast Line to Havelock (TE16), which lies at the north edge of the estate; or Downtown Line to Chinatown, then North East Line to Outram Park and East-West Line one stop. On public-holiday Monday 9 November, Tiong Bahru Market's hawker stalls may be partly closed and cafes will be busy; arrive by 8.30am.
- Combining Tiong Bahru (morning) with the Southern Ridges on the same Monday is too much in heat. If both stay on 9 November, start the Southern Ridges at 7.30am and do Tiong Bahru for a late lunch, since Tiong Bahru is one Circle Line and East-West Line connection from HarbourFront.
- Little India and Deepavali: Deepavali falls on Sunday 8 November, during their stay. Little India is the centre of the festival, with the Serangoon Road street light-up, a crowded festive bazaar in the weeks before, and temples full on the day. By ruling out Little India they would miss Singapore's main Deepavali spectacle; the light-up is best seen on an evening shortly before 8 November.
