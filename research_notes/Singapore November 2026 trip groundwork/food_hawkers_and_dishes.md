# Singapore food reference for 2 to 9 November 2026: hawker centres, named stalls, dishes, chains and a special dinner

Research date: 25 October 2026. Travellers are based at Hillview MRT (DT3, Downtown Line), 510 Upper Bukit Timah Road, S678211, 1.36234, 103.76742 (OneMap; Exit A 1.36250, 103.76769).

Method notes for the report writer:
- Coordinates come from OneMap, the Singapore Land Authority's national address search (public endpoint `onemap.gov.sg/api/common/elastic/search`), queried on 25 October 2026. They mark the building or address point. OneMap does not locate individual stalls, so a stall's coordinate is its hawker centre's coordinate. Where OneMap gave only a road-level or nearby point, I say so.
- Walking times are my own estimates: straight-line distance from OneMap station coordinates, multiplied by 1.3 for street detours, at 70 m per minute. They are inferences, not sourced figures.
- Scheduled cleaning closures come from the NEA dataset on data.gov.sg ("Dates of Hawker Centres Closure", resource `d_bda4baa634dd1cc7a6c7cad5f19e2d68`, 123 centres, January to December 2026), pulled on 25 October 2026. Source: [data.gov.sg NEA closure dataset](https://data.gov.sg/api/action/datastore_search?resource_id=d_bda4baa634dd1cc7a6c7cad5f19e2d68&limit=200).
- Public holidays during the trip: Deepavali falls on Sunday 8 November 2026, and Monday 9 November 2026 is the public holiday in lieu — [Circles.Life holiday guide](https://www.circles.life/sg/blog/holiday-guide-sg-public-holidays-november-2026/); [Wego](https://blog.wego.com/deepavali-2026-singapore-travel-plan/). These are secondary sources that cite MOM. Many family stalls close on public holidays, so 9 November is a risk day.
- Food-blog verdicts (Eatbook, Seth Lui, Daniel Food Diary, Miss Tam Chiak, HungryGoWhere, Burpple, Time Out) are opinion and are marked as such.

## Q1. Hawker centres and the stalls the travellers named

### Takeaway
None of the travellers' hawker centres has an NEA cleaning closure between 2 and 9 November 2026. The nearest closures fall just outside the trip: Golden Mile closes 26 to 29 October, Tiong Bahru on 16 November, and Chinatown Complex on 16 and 17 November. Each named dish has a clear stall to go to. Opening days, though, are the main risk: several key stalls close on Sunday, Tuesday or Wednesday, and some close early or when sold out.

#### 1. Maxwell Food Centre
- Address and coordinates: 1 Kadayanallur Street, S069184; 1.28033, 103.84475 (OneMap 1.280331427, 103.844747227).
- Nearest MRT: Maxwell (TE18, Thomson-East Coast Line), 1.28053, 103.84388, about 2 minutes on foot (estimate). From Hillview, take the DTL to Stevens (DT10), then the TEL to Maxwell. - Centre hours: sources conflict. - Rojak, popiah and cockles: Rojak, Popiah & Cockle, #01-56. A 2018 guide calls it the only rojak and popiah stall at Maxwell, and Michelin's guide, updated December 2025, still lists it and recommends the made-to-order popiah. Cockles are sold only on selected days. — [CityNomads](https://citynomads.com/maxwell-food-centre-hawker-guide-stalls-you-need-to-try-while-theyre-still-around/); [Michelin best hawker centres](https://guide.michelin.com/ae-du/en/best-of/the-best-hawker-centers-in-singapore-and-what-to-order); [HungryGoWhere Maxwell](https://hungrygowhere.com/what-to-eat/maxwell-food-centre/)
- Fuzhou oyster cake: Maxwell Fuzhou Oyster Cake, #01-05. Open 9am to 8pm, closed Sunday. Price is S$2 to S$2.50 per cake (sources differ), and S$1 more buys extra oysters. The stall dates to 1962 or 1964 (sources differ), is run by the second generation (Mrs Hoon Poh Hwa, her husband and son Mohan Deitrich), and is Michelin-listed. The cakes are fried to order with oysters, prawns and minced meat in a rice batter. — [Seth Lui](https://sethlui.com/maxwell-fuzhou-oyster-cake-singapore/); [HungryGoWhere](https://hungrygowhere.com/critics-reviews/maxwell-fuzhou-oyster-cake); [Michelin](https://guide.michelin.com/th/en/singapore-region/singapore/restaurant/maxwell-fuzhou-oyster-cake)
- Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice (#01-10/11) holds a 2026 Bib Gourmand. Michelin calls it worth the wait and suggests half a chicken to share. — [Eatbook Bib Gourmand 2026](https://eatbook.sg/michelin-bib-gourmand-2026/); [Michelin](https://guide.michelin.com/kr/ko/singapore-region/singapore/restaurant/tian-tian-hainanese-chicken-rice)
- Tian Tian queues run about 10 minutes at opening (10am) or mid-afternoon and about 45 minutes at meal times, according to a Burpple reviewer. A travel site puts the peak at 45 to 90 minutes between 11:30am and 1:30pm. The same Burpple reviewer said the difference is subtle enough that it may not be worth the queue for people who are not chicken-rice purists (opinion). — [Burpple](https://www.burpple.com/f/uJSnphBv); [Pickyourtrail](https://pickyourtrail.com/attractions/singapore/maxwell-food-center-singapore)

### Inferences
- The best day plan for Maxwell is a weekday other than Tuesday, arriving around 10am. That suits Tian Tian's shorter queue, avoids Ah Tai's Tuesday rest day (Q5) and the oyster cake's Sunday closure, and leaves Amoy Street (about 300 m away) for an early curry puff on the same morning. J2 sells out and has listed weekday-only hours.
- Golden Mile char siu works only before about 2pm and not on Wednesday.
- Old Airport Road, the Katong cluster and Changi Village are the trips that cost the most time from Hillview. Grouping them into one east-side day would help.
- For "Akka", the travellers should be asked whether they meant KK Hospital, Alexandra (Hospital or Village Food Centre), or a specific video. A hospital canteen is a weak use of trip time unless they have a particular stall in mind.

### Gaps
- No official NEA stall-level hours were available. Stall hours come from blogs and vary by year.
- Air-conditioning: I found no source this session confirming air-conditioning status for these centres. My understanding is that all of them are naturally ventilated, with fans, but this needs confirmation.
- Crowd timing by centre (busy periods, quietest days) is not documented beyond Tian Tian's queues and Amoy Street's reputation as a CBD lunch centre. I did not verify the claim that Amoy Street is quiet at weekends.
- Tian Tian's unit number (#01-10/11) and current price were not confirmed this session.
- I found no source naming a best satay stall at Lau Pa Sat by name, and no current crab availability there.
- No source identified "Akka hospital canteen".

## Q2. Specific dishes: where to go for each

Most dishes have a clear, well-regarded option on or near the travellers' list. "Oats pancake" almost certainly means Aunty Oats Pancake, a min jiang kueh stall at Old Airport Road.
