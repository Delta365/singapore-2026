# Itemised budget for a couple's Singapore trip, 2 to 9 November 2026 (mid-range)

Scope note: researched 25 October 2026. Two adults on Indian passports, staying at Hillview (DT3), accommodation already paid. All figures are for the couple unless marked "pp" (per person). Prices are in Singapore dollars and converted at **S$1 = ₹74.10** (mid-market, 22 October 2026, [Currency.wiki](https://currency.wiki/sgd-inr)). Banks and forex cards will give a slightly worse rate, so a 1 to 2 percent buffer on INR figures is sensible.

**Itinerary basis (revised by the coordinator on 25 October 2026 after flights were confirmed).** The inbound flight lands at Changi on the evening of Mon 2 Nov; the outbound flight departs Changi T3 early on Mon 9 Nov. Day 1 is arrival only (optional hour at Jewel, taxi or Grab to Hillview, supper at The Rail Mall). Tiong Bahru Market and estate move to Day 2 before Chinatown. Amoy Street Food Centre and Ya Kun move to the start of Day 6. Day 8 is a 04:30 pre-booked taxi to T3 only. The Southern Ridges and Henderson Waves become optional extras. Dairy Farm Nature Park is dropped from Day 1 because of the night arrival.

**Convention used throughout.** "Cited" means a price taken from the linked source. "Estimate" means my own figure built from cited inputs or, where no source was found, my judgement. Estimates are labelled.

**Three facts that change the plan:**
1. **NUS Baba House is closed for restoration**, reopening in 2027 per its own visitor page (an NUS Alumni page says 2028). The optional Day 2 Baba House tour cannot be booked. [NUS Baba House: Plan your visit](https://babahouse.nus.edu.sg/plan-your-visit/); [NUS AlumNet](https://nus.edu.sg/alumnet/events/results/blair-plain-neighbourhood-tour-by-nus-baba-house)
2. **Cloud Forest closes on 26 October 2026 for maintenance**, a single-day closure before the trip. The next Flower Dome closure is 16 November 2026, after the trip. Both conservatories should be open on Sat 7 Nov. [Gardens by the Bay: Flower Dome](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/flower-dome.html); [Gardens by the Bay: Cloud Forest](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/cloud-forest.html)
3. **Both airport taxis fall inside surcharge windows.** The arrival (about 21:00) is in the 5:00pm to 11:59pm peak window with the S$8 airport surcharge. The 04:30 departure is in the 50 percent midnight window. See the transport section.

## Attraction prices in 2026 for two adults, with resident and tourist pricing and discounts

### Takeaway
The paid items on the core plan total S$290 for two: Orchid Garden S$30, Zoo S$98, National Gallery S$40 and the two Gardens by the Bay conservatories S$92. Singapore residents pay less at every paid venue, and the couple pays the standard or non-resident rate. The biggest possible saving is on Gardens by the Bay through Klook, but the claimed discount comes from an affiliate blog and needs checking on the day.

### Cited Findings

**National Orchid Garden (Day 3)**
- Standard (non-local) adult S$15; local adult S$5; seniors and students S$3 standard and S$1 local; children under 12 free. Open 8.30am to 7pm, last ticket and entry 6pm. [NParks: National Orchid Garden](https://sbg.nparks.gov.sg/attractions/national-orchid-garden/); [NParks Visitor Information](https://sbg.nparks.gov.sg/visit/general-info/)
- The local rate covers citizens, PRs and pass holders living in Singapore, so tourists pay S$15. [NParks: National Orchid Garden](https://sbg.nparks.gov.sg/attractions/national-orchid-garden/)
- Klook lists a foreigner ticket at S$15.00, the same as the gate price. [Klook](https://www.klook.com/en-SG/activity/133225-national-orchid-garden-singapore/)
- The rest of the Singapore Botanic Gardens is free. Search results confirm only the Orchid Garden is ticketed. [NParks Visitor Information](https://sbg.nparks.gov.sg/visit/general-info/)

**Gardens by the Bay (Day 6)**
- Non-resident Flower Dome and Cloud Forest (featuring Jurassic World: The Experience) combined: **S$46 adult**, S$32 child. Residents pay S$34. Prices include GST. [Gardens by the Bay: Flower Dome](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/flower-dome.html); [Gardens by the Bay: Cloud Forest](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/cloud-forest.html)
- There is no non-resident single-dome ticket. Residents can buy Flower Dome only at S$12 or Cloud Forest only at S$26. [Gardens by the Bay: Flower Dome](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/flower-dome.html)
- Non-resident bundle (Flower Dome, Cloud Forest, Disney Garden of Wonder at Floral Fantasy and shuttle): the official page shows two unlabelled figures, S$73 and S$58. Residents' equivalent shows S$49 and S$42. [Gardens by the Bay: Flower Dome](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/flower-dome.html)
- Flower Dome hours: daily 9.00am to 9.00pm, last admission 8.30pm. [Gardens by the Bay: Flower Dome](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/flower-dome.html)
- "Cloud Forest will be closed on 26 Oct 2026 for Maintenance." The next Flower Dome closure is 16 Nov 2026. [Gardens by the Bay: Cloud Forest](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/cloud-forest.html)
- A 2026 affiliate travel blog quotes the combined ticket at about S$29 on Klook against S$46 at the gate, roughly 30 percent cheaper. The blog earns commission from Klook. [Trvlogue](https://www.trvlogue.com/en/gardens-by-the-bay-tickets)
- Other resellers vary widely: Expedia about $52, Travelocity $51.09, a YTS Holidays listing $33, AAA TripCanvas from $25.81. Currencies and contents differ. [Expedia](https://www.expedia.com/things-to-do/gardens-by-the-bay-tickets-cloud-forest-flower-dome.a50849957.activity-details); [AAA TripCanvas](https://www.aaa.com/tripcanvas/thing-to-do/gardens-by-the-bay-ticket-40856P20)

**Mandai: Singapore Zoo and Night Safari (Day 4)**
- Non-resident adult (regular days): Singapore Zoo **S$49**, Night Safari **S$58**, Bird Paradise S$49, River Wonders S$45. [Mandai: Single attraction tickets](https://www.mandai.com/en/tickets-and-passes/single-attractions.html)
- Resident adult: Zoo S$39 regular and S$44 peak; Night Safari S$48 regular and S$52 peak. [Mandai: Single attraction tickets](https://www.mandai.com/en/tickets-and-passes/single-attractions.html)

Thursday 5 November is therefore a regular day for both, but the page shows non-resident prices for regular days only. [Mandai: Single attraction tickets](https://www.mandai.com/en/tickets-and-passes/single-attractions.html)

Choices include Zoo, Night Safari, River Wonders, Bird Paradise and others. Prices include 9 percent GST. [Mandai: Multi-attraction passes](https://www.mandai.com/en/tickets-and-passes/multi-attractions.html)
- Mandai advertises a resident WildPass Zoo price from S$30.60 and a 50 percent POSB cardmember discount, neither of which applies to Indian visitors. [Mandai: Promotions](https://www.mandai.com/en/tickets-and-passes/promotions.html)

### Inferences

**GST and service charge ("plus plus")**
- GST is 9 percent from 1 January 2024. Service charge, typically 10 percent, is itself subject to GST. IRAS's worked example: S$100 food plus S$10 service charge plus S$9.90 GST equals **S$119.90**, so "++" prices are multiplied by 1.199. [IRAS: Hotel and F&B](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-(gst)/specific-business-sectors/hotel-and-food-beverage)
- Normally prices must be displayed GST-inclusive. Only hotels and F&B outlets that levy a service charge may display GST-exclusive prices, and they must prominently state that prices are subject to GST and service charge. [IRAS: Displaying and quoting prices](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/goods-services-tax-(gst)/basics-of-gst/invoicing-price-display-and-record-keeping/displaying-and-quoting-prices)

### Inferences
- **Hawker centres, kopitiams and fast food chains** levy no service charge, so the price shown is the price paid. Sit-down restaurants at Dempsey Hill, Jumbo, Long Beach or Candlenut will almost all add "++", so add about 20 percent to every menu price.
- **Special dinner costing (estimates):**
  - Peranakan (Candlenut or similar), à la carte for two without alcohol: about S$90 to S$100 before charges, so **S$110 to S$120** after ++. The S$118++ pp figure with drinks would take it to about S$280.
  - Chilli crab (Jumbo at Riverside Point fits the Clarke Quay evening on Day 6): a 1.2 kg crab at S$100 per kg is S$120, plus mantou, a vegetable, rice and two drinks about S$50 to S$60, so S$175 before charges and **about S$210 to S$220** after ++. A larger crab or premium crab pushes it to S$280.
  - A Michelin Bib Gourmand stall or casual restaurant: no current prices were collected; in this budget it is treated as a normal hawker or casual meal at S$20 to S$60 for two.
- **Local chains (estimates, no 2026 prices found):** McDonald's or KFC meal S$8 to S$10 each; MOS Burger S$9 to S$12; Shake Shack burger with fries S$15 to S$20 each; Toast Box set S$6 to S$7; 7-Eleven snack and drink S$3 to S$6. Swapping a hawker meal for a chain meal adds about S$5 to S$15 for two, which fits inside the expected food budget.

## Transport: MRT and bus spend, Grab allowance and airport transfers

### Takeaway
Public transport for the six touring days is about S$100 for two including the daily card fee. The two airport taxis cost more than all the MRT riding put together, at about S$135 for the pair of trips, because both fall in surcharge windows. Budget about S$45 for two ordinary Grab rides plus the Grab legs on the Zoo day.

### Cited Findings
- Adult card fares S$1.28 (up to 3.2 km) to S$2.57 (over 40.2 km), effective 27 December 2025. The next increase takes effect 26 December 2026, after the trip. [PTC fare structure](https://www.ptc.gov.sg/fare-regulation/bus-rail/fare-structure); [PTC: Fare Review Exercise 2025](https://www.ptc.gov.sg/media-centre/newsroom/fare-review-exercise-2025/)
- Selected bands: 3.3 to 4.2 km S$1.38; 5.3 to 6.2 km S$1.59; 9.3 to 10.2 km S$1.86; 13.3 to 14.2 km S$2.02; 16.3 to 17.2 km S$2.15; 19.3 to 20.2 km S$2.27; 24.3 to 25.2 km S$2.40. [PTC fare structure](https://www.ptc.gov.sg/fare-regulation/bus-rail/fare-structure)
- "A SGD0.60 Foreign Bank Card Admin Fee per day will apply for foreign-issued Mastercard or Visa debit/credit cards." [SimplyGo FAQ](https://simplygo.com.sg/faqs/cards-and-charms/simplygo/contactless-bank-cards/)

### Inferences
- **Arrival taxi, Mon 2 Nov about 21:00 (estimate).** The companion notes estimate the base meter for Changi to Hillview at roughly S$37 to S$55. Adding the 25 percent evening peak and the S$8 airport surcharge gives **S$54 to S$77**. The Concierge S$70 fixed fare sits near the top of that range but removes any surge risk on Grab. Expected S$65.
- **Departure taxi, Mon 9 Nov at 04:30 (estimate).** The 50 percent midnight surcharge applies, so S$37 to S$55 becomes **S$56 to S$83**, plus any advance-booking fee. The S$6 to S$8 airport surcharge applies to pick-ups at Changi, so it should not apply to a drop-off. The day being a public holiday does not change the midnight surcharge. Expected S$70. A Grab quote booked the evening before is worth comparing.
- **MRT and bus by day (estimates from the fare table, per person, including the S$0.60 fee):** Day 2 S$7.51, Day 3 S$7.99, Day 5 S$7.99, Day 6 S$8.89, Day 7 S$9.52, Day 4 about S$2.60 for the bus home. Total about S$45 pp, or S$90 for two, plus S$9.60 of admin fees already inside those numbers. A Tourist Pass would cost more (S$45 plus S$17 or more for six days of use).
- **Grab allowance (estimate).** A Hillview to city ride of about 20 km meters at about S$18, rising to about S$23 in the evening peak. The expected case includes one late ride home on Day 6 (S$22) and two Grab legs on Day 4 (about S$40). The high case adds a ride on most evenings.

## Currency and payment for Indian travellers

### Takeaway
The rate was about ₹74.10 per S$1 on 22 October 2026, down from about ₹75.6 in early October. The cheapest way to pay is a zero-markup card (credit or prepaid forex) for transit, chains and restaurants, with S$150 to S$250 in cash for hawker stalls, bumboats and small shops. UPI works in Singapore only in a limited way and should not be relied on.

### Cited Findings
- **Rate:** 1 SGD = ₹74.1030 on 22 October 2026 (Currency.wiki); ₹74.186 the same day (Exchange-rates.org); ₹73.61 to ₹73.94 on 21 October (OFX, Currency.me.uk). The 30-day range was ₹73.75 to ₹75.62, with an average of ₹74.84 and a fall of 1.51 percent. [Currency.wiki](https://currency.wiki/sgd-inr); [Exchange-rates.org](https://www.exchange-rates.org/converter/sgd-inr); [OFX](https://www.ofx.com/en-us/exchange-rates/sgd-to-inr/)
- **Zero-markup cards from India:** Niyo Global (SBM Bank, prepaid Visa) is zero forex markup and lifetime free; Scapia (Federal Bank) is a lifetime-free zero-markup credit card. Some cards marketed as zero-markup actually charge 1.5 to 3.5 percent, so check before travelling. [Jupiter](https://jupiter.money/blog/zero-forex-markup-credit-card/); [CardAdvisor](https://cardadvisor.in/articles/forex-card-vs-zero-forex-credit-card-india-2026); [Happyfares](https://happyfares.in/blog/best-forex-cards-india-2026/)
- **SimplyGo transit:** Visa, Mastercard and Amex contactless cards work at MRT gates and on buses; Maestro and Cirrus do not. [SimplyGo FAQ](https://simplygo.com.sg/faqs/cards-and-charms/simplygo/contactless-bank-cards/)
- **Money changers:** Mustafa Centre is open 24 hours and usually gives the best INR rate. The Arcade at Raffles Place has many changers side by side and handles INR, but is strongest for major currencies. Rates vary by changer and amount. [Travjoy 2026](https://travjoy.com/blog/singapore-money-exchange-in-singapore-where-to-get-the-best-rates-2026); [Niva Bupa](https://www.nivabupa.com/travel-insurance-articles/foreign-exchange-singapore-money-changers-rates-tips.html)
- **Hawker payments:** Many stalls display PayNow or SGQR codes; Alipay+ via SGQR covered about 11,000 stalls (date unconfirmed). No 2026 source confirms foreign card acceptance at hawker stalls. [Trip.com](https://sg.trip.com/guide/payments/best-payment-methods-in-singapore.html); [Fintech News SG](https://fintechnews.sg/71856/payments/alipay-integrates-with-sgqr-to-enable-qr-payments-at-11000-hawkers/)
- **UPI:** The UPI to PayNow link went live in February 2023. Sources conflict: some say Indian UPI apps can scan PayNow QR codes widely, while a travel blog says the link is mainly person-to-person and that standard PayNow merchant codes often fail, reporting a 15 to 20 percent success rate. None of the sources are official. [MoneyView](https://moneyview.in/upi/upi-accepted-countries); [TripCabinet](https://tripcabinet.com/blog/upi-singapore-payment-guide); [HitPay](https://hitpayapp.com/blog/how-to-accept-upi-payments-singapore)

### Inferences
- **Recommended setup:** each traveller carries one zero-markup Visa or Mastercard for transit (one card per person, tapped consistently), plus a backup card from a different network. Take about S$200 in cash from India or change INR at Mustafa, which also suits Day 7 (Kampong Glam is close to Little India).
- Hawker stalls are the main cash need. A day of hawker food at S$60 to S$90 for two should be assumed to be cash unless a card or QR sign is visible.
- On a S$1,670 spend, a 3.5 percent forex markup costs about S$58 (₹4,300). A zero-markup card saves most of that.
- Enable international usage in the UPI app and test it on a small payment, but treat any success as a bonus.

### Gaps
- No official NPCI, MAS or NETS statement on UPI merchant acceptance in Singapore in 2026 was found.
- No live INR buy and sell rates from Mustafa or The Arcade were obtained.

## Where to save and where not to skimp, for this plan

### Takeaway
The best savings are on the two airport taxis, Gardens by the Bay tickets, and the location of everyday meals. Spend on the special dinner, the Zoo and the National Gallery, which are central to the trip and have no cheap substitute.

### Cited Findings
- Gardens by the Bay combined ticket S$46 at the gate against about S$29 reported on Klook. [Gardens by the Bay](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/en/things-to-do/attractions/flower-dome.html); [Trvlogue](https://www.trvlogue.com/en/gardens-by-the-bay-tickets)
- Chicken rice costs S$4.50 to S$6 in heartland centres against S$5.50 to S$8 at Maxwell. [JNP Travel](https://jnptravel.com/blog/singapore-hawker-food-price-guide-2026)
- GoCycling's second hour has been free on weekdays when riding starts before 5pm. [TheSmartLocal](https://thesmartlocal.com/read/bicycle-rental-singapore/)
- Peak and midnight taxi surcharges are 25 and 50 percent. [Changi Airport](https://www.changiairport.com/en/airport-guide/transport/leaving-the-airport.html)

### Inferences
**Where to save (estimates of the saving for two):**
- Check Klook or KKday for Gardens by the Bay a week ahead: up to about S$34.
- Compare a Grab quote with the S$70 Concierge fare on arrival, and book the 04:30 departure on Grab the night before if it quotes under the metered estimate: S$10 to S$20.
- Eat breakfast and some dinners near Hillview (The Rail Mall, HillV2) instead of in tourist centres: S$5 to S$10 a meal.
- Start the East Coast Park bikes before 5pm on Friday for the free hour: about S$20.
- Skip the Night Safari unless it is a priority; it adds about S$160 with dinner and the late ride.
- Use a zero-markup card: about S$58 compared with a 3.5 percent card.
- Bring N95 masks and refillable bottles from India: S$10 to S$30.

**Where not to skimp:**
- The special dinner. It is the one planned splurge, and choosing a reputable crab or Peranakan restaurant matters more than saving S$50.
- The Zoo. S$98 for two is a fair price for a full day, and the Sungei Buloh to Zoo Grab is worth paying for to save an hour of buses in the heat.
- Late rides home. A S$20 to S$25 Grab after a long Day 6 is better value than a long MRT ride at closing time, and much cheaper than a post-midnight taxi.
- Travel insurance, at about ₹1,000 per person.

### Gaps
- The Klook saving is from an affiliate source and was not verified against the live Klook price.
