## 1. East Coast Park cycling

### Takeaway
Hire from GoCycling C4 (8am to 10pm, from S$10 per hour, tandem from S$20 per hour, S$50 cash deposit). East Coast Park runs about 13 km and is on the Round Island Route. There is no adjacent MRT, so use bus 401 on weekends or walk through an underpass from Marine Parade Road.

### Cited Findings
- GoCycling rates: adult S$10 per hour, kids S$8, childseat S$16, tandem S$20, family bike S$40 (C4 only in ECP). Night rental from 6pm costs from S$20 plus a S$2 lock. A S$50 cash deposit applies. The latest hourly return is 30 minutes before closing. [GoCycling](https://www.gocycling.sg/)
- GoCycling C4 is at 1030 East Coast Parkway (1.30233, 103.91795), open 8am to 10pm. G1 is at 1490 East Coast Parkway (1.31412, 103.96019), self-service, 10am to 7pm. [GoCycling](https://www.gocycling.sg/); [OneMap]
- Coastline Leisure also rents from S$10 per hour. Some kiosks offer a free hour before 5pm. [The Money Bees](https://themoneybees.co/blog/bicycle-rental-singapore)
- The park covers 180 ha over more than 13 km. It is on the Eastern Coastal Loop and the Round Island Route. Areas run A to H, with the Cyclist Park in Area D. NParks names Bedok MRT as the nearest station. [NParks](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/park-detail/east-coast-park)
- OneMap points: Area A 1.29444, 103.87254; Area C 1.29928, 103.90784; Area D 1.30433, 103.92476; East Coast Lagoon Food Village 1.30726, 103.93480. [OneMap]
- Bus 401 runs from Bedok Interchange into the park on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays only. On weekdays, take bus 36, 48 or 196 to Marine Parade Road and walk through an underpass. [Land Transport Guru](https://landtransportguru.net/bus401/); [Lonely Planet](https://www.nonprod.lonelyplanet.com/points-of-interest/east-coast-park/1225209)
- The Round Island Route is planned as a 150 km loop by 2035. Phase 1, a 75 km stretch from Rower's Bay to Berlayer Creek, opened in January 2022. The Coast-to-Coast Trail is 36 km, from Jurong Lake Gardens to Coney Island. [NParks](https://nparks.gov.sg/news/2022/1/nparks-completes-phase-one-of-the-round-island-route-with-75km-of-recreational-connection-stretching-across-eastern-half-of-singapore); [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/singapore/things-to-do/the-ultimate-guide-to-the-coast-to-coast-trail)
- Changi Village (1.38871, 103.98798) and its hawker centre (1.38915, 103.98825) are at the end of the eastern coastal route. [OneMap]
- Anywheel works only through its app: scan to ride, then park in a designated area and scan its QR code. Passes cost S$3.90 for 7 days, S$9.90 for 30 days and S$23 for 90 days. Rides are capped at 30 minutes, then S$0.50 per extra 30 minutes. [Anywheel](https://www.anywheel.sg/); [SingSaver](https://www.singsaver.com.sg/blog/bicycle-rental-singapore)

### Inferences
- On Fri 6 Nov (a weekday), bus 401 does not run. Take bus 36, 48 or 196 to Marine Parade, then walk through an underpass to Area C or D, near C4.
- Cycling from C4 to Marina Bay (about 8 km west through Area A) is feasible. Changi Village is about 15 km east, which is a long ride on a hired bike that has to come back to C4.
- C4 to Katong (GV Katong 1.30522, 103.90510) is a short hop inland. This suits the plan.

### Gaps
- A daily rate and Coastline Leisure kiosk coordinates were not found.
- Whether Anywheel accepts foreign cards and phone numbers is unconfirmed.
- The exact underpass locations were not geocoded.
