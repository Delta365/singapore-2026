## 1. Tanglin Crispy Curry Puff: which hawker centre, unit, hours, rest day

### Takeaway
It is at Hong Lim Market & Food Centre, but there are two rival family stalls side by side. The one branded "Since 1952" is reported at #02-34 and opens Monday to Saturday 7am to 4pm, closed Sunday. Go on Tue 3 Nov in the morning.

### Cited Findings
- Two stalls, one unit apart, after a split between brothers: #02-34 "Tanglin Crispy Curry Puff Since 1952" (Ray Ng) and #02-36 "Tanglin Crispy Curry Puff Original" (Peter Ng). Source: [Eatbook](https://eatbook.sg/underrated-curry-puff/); [HungryOnion thread](https://www.hungryonion.org/t/singapore-tanglin-crispy-curry-puff-rivals-at-hong-lim-food-centre/32785)
- #02-34 hours: Monday to Saturday 7am to 4pm, phone 8418 3937, most sources agree. Source: [Eatbook](https://eatbook.sg/underrated-curry-puff/); [Live2Makan, Oct 2024](https://live2makan.com/2024/10/01/tanglin-crispy-curry-puff-hong-lim/)
- #02-36 hours conflict: one guide says Wednesday to Monday 7.30am to 4pm, another says Monday to Saturday 8am to 3pm, closed Sunday. Source: [Miss Tam Chiak](https://misstamchiak.com/best-curry-puff-singapore/); [HungryGoWhere](https://hungrygowhere.com/what-to-eat/curry-puff-singapore/)
- Hong Lim Food Centre and Market: 531A Upper Cross Street, Singapore 051531, 1.285324, 103.845777. Source: [OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)

### Inferences
- The S$2 per puff price from the video is plausible for 2025 to 2026 but I found no dated source confirming it. Classic chicken curry puff and sardine puff are the usual orders.
- Verdict: a reliable old-school snack and an easy stop 200 m from Chinatown MRT; go before noon as puffs sell out.

### Gaps
- No 2026-dated confirmation of hours or price. Which of the two stalls the video showed depends on the sign; the "since 1952" wording points to #02-34.

## 2. Bee Cheng Hiang bak kwa: outlets, price per 500 g, carrying into India

### Takeaway
Two Chinatown shops are confirmed (69 Pagoda Street, 189 New Bridge Road), both next to Chinatown MRT and on the Tue 3 Nov route. Price for classic minced pork was about S$29.50 to S$35 per 500 g in 2024 to 2025. I found no official Indian rule for passengers carrying bak kwa; commercial imports of pork products need a DAHD Sanitary Import Permit, so the travellers should declare it at Mumbai customs or skip it.

### Cited Findings
- Pagoda Street outlet: 69 Pagoda Street, Singapore 059228, daily 9am to 8pm. Source: [Chinatown.sg](https://chinatown.sg/shop/bee-cheng-hiang-pagoda/)
- New Bridge Road outlet: 189 New Bridge Road, Singapore 059422, 9am to 10pm. Source: [Chinatown.sg](https://chinatown.sg/shop/bee-cheng-hiang-new-bridge/)
- Coordinates: 69 Pagoda St 1.283522, 103.843779; 189 New Bridge Rd 1.284478, 103.843999. Source: [OneMap](https://www.onemap.gov.sg/)
- Changi: Bee Cheng Hiang opened in the T2 departure check-in hall; the T3 check-in hall outlet named in the same report is Fragrance Bak Kwa, a different brand. An older T3 airside concession ran from 2011 for three years. Source: [Inside Retail](https://insideretail.asia/?p=25779); [Moodie Davitt](https://moodiedavittreport.com/bee-cheng-hiang-captures-changi-t3-delicatessen-contract)
- Price: classic minced pork S$29.50 per 500 g, sliced S$35 per 500 g (article carries a January 2024 disclaimer). Source: [SingSaver](https://www.singsaver.com.sg/page/blog/best-bak-kwa-to-buy-for-cny-in-singapore)
- Price: regular minced S$70 per kg, sliced S$72, chilli S$76 (CNY 2024, so S$35 to S$38 per 500 g, likely CNY-season pricing). Source: [MoneySmart](https://blog.moneysmart.sg/budgeting/bak-kwa-prices)
- India: integrated veterinary health certificates and DAHD Sanitary Import Permits are required for commercial import of pork and pork products. Source: [ChemLinked](https://food.chemlinked.com/foodpedia/india-meat-regulation); [NACIN e-book](https://www.nacin.gov.in/ZCLucknow/Images/Documents/E_Books/42_Import%20of%20Livestocks%20&%20Livestock%20products.pdf)
- A third-party traveller site says sealed processed pork in personal quantities is usually tolerated if declared, while fresh pork is confiscated (not official). Source: [CanIBringTo](https://www.canibringto.com/can-i-bring/meat-products-to-india/)

### Inferences
- Buy on Tue 3 Nov in Chinatown if they want it, but buy the vacuum-sealed packs (Bee Cheng Hiang sells these) and declare them in Mumbai. Plain loose bak kwa in a paper bag is the most likely to be seized.
- Verdict: the safest-known brand to buy in Chinatown; the India import question is the real risk, not the shop.

### Gaps
- Orchard and Bugis outlets were not confirmed in this research; check the store locator at beechenghiang.com.sg.
- No current (Oct 2026) non-CNY price found.
- No official CBIC or DAHD passenger-baggage allowance for meat products was found. Treat the "tolerated if declared" claim as unverified.
- Whether any Bee Cheng Hiang shop is in Terminal 3 (landside or airside) in Nov 2026 is unconfirmed.
