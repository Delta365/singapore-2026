# Singapore, 2 to 9 November 2026

A travel plan for a couple on Indian passports, based near Hillview MRT on the Downtown Line, with six full days in Singapore. The days follow the agreed plan in `itinerary-current.json`. Everything else is drawn from the research notes in `research_notes/Singapore November 2026 trip groundwork/`, compiled on 24 and 25 October 2026.

The pace is relaxed, with two or three main stops a day, early starts before the heat, a rest in the afternoon and an indoor or evening stop to finish. The interests behind the plan are hawker food, bakeries and dessert; nature and walking; and culture, museums and heritage architecture.

## Before you go

- Saturday 31 October (Singapore time): each of you submits the SG Arrival Card through the ICA e-Service or the MyICA app. It is free.
- Book: Keng Eng Kee for Wednesday dinner (+65 6272 1038), Sun with Moon for Thursday dinner, and timed entry to the Gardens by the Bay domes for Saturday (check Klook first). Book the 04:30 ComfortDelGro taxi on the evening of Sunday 8 November.
- Optional bookings: the Saturday 9:30am Fort Canning walking tour, the Food Playground cooking class, the SSO on Friday, CATS on Saturday.
- Cards: a zero-markup Visa or Mastercard each, enabled for international contactless payments, plus a backup. For the iPhone, raise the buyer's international limit to at least S$2,200.
- Cash: about S$200.
- Pack N95 masks, Type G adapters, repellent, an umbrella and clothes that cover shoulders and knees.
- From 30 October: check the PSI each morning (NEA or myENV). Around 31 October: recheck the weather forecast and the MSS fortnightly outlook.
- Check the LTA newsroom for any Downtown Line works announced for early November.

## Where the notes and the plan disagree

The plan is followed throughout. These are the points where a research note says something different, so you know which figure to trust.

- The budget notes priced an earlier draft of the plan: a S$220 special dinner on Day 6, a Dempsey lunch and Newton dinner on Day 3, Amoy Street on Saturday, a Grab between Sungei Buloh and the Zoo, and a Rail Mall supper on Day 1. `budget_summary.md` keeps their tables and adds a re-mapped estimate for the agreed plan.
- Kranji to Khatib on Day 4: the plan says five stops on the North South Line; it is seven.
- Arrival taxi: the local intelligence notes estimate S$40 to S$50; the plan and the budget notes use S$55 to S$75, which allows for the distance west to Hillview.
- Departure taxi: the plan says about S$60; the budget notes estimate S$56 to S$83 plus any booking fee.
- Funan: the activities notes found no cinema there and named GV Capitol as the nearest; the plan and the budget notes put GV Funan inside the mall (a Deluxe Plus site, which may cost more than the standard S$16).
- Victoria Theatre and Concert Hall, a Day 6 option: no production was found for Saturday 7 November. The nearest is "Classic Memories" on Friday 6 November.
- Heap Seng Leong on Day 7: one source says it closes at 4pm, which is tight after the 2pm mosque visit.
- Changi Chapel and Museum: S$9 on the museum's own page; the budget notes used an older S$8.
- Food Playground: S$120 nett each on its own page; the budget notes estimated about US$116 to US$118 each from resellers.
- LANY: one listing gives tickets from S$138; the budget notes found no face value.
- The Zoo: the plan allows three to four hours; the sites notes suggest four to five.
- Birds of Paradise: the plan names pandan and strawberry basil gelato; the notes name white chrysanthemum and lychee raspberry as the signatures.
- Older statements in the notes that no longer apply: the visa timing warning (the visas are done), the speculation about an "Akka" hospital canteen (it became the NUS cafeterias), the note that the Kokuyo shop name was unverified (it is at 31 and 33 Ann Siang Road), and a suggestion to walk the Southern Ridges on 9 November (Day 8 is the flight home).
