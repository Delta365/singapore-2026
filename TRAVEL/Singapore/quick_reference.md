## SG Arrival Card

Each of you must submit one. The window opens on Saturday 31 October (Singapore time), three days before arrival. It is free, through the ICA SG Arrival Card e-Service or the MyICA app only. The e-Pass comes by email. Ignore any site that charges a fee.

## Rules that catch visitors

- No eating or drinking on the MRT, buses, stations or bus interchanges, including water. Up to S$500.
- Return your tray and litter at hawker centres.
- No vapes or e-cigarettes. Up to S$10,000.
- No durians on the MRT.
- No duty-free cigarettes; bring none.
- Temples and the mosque: shoulders and knees covered, shoes off where asked.
- Kampong Lorong Buangkok: stay on the lane, keep quiet, no photographs of homes.
