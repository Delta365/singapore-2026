# Your base: Hillview

Your accommodation is already booked near Hillview MRT, Downtown Line DT3, so this file does not recommend hotels. It covers what is around the station: food, shops, late-night options, nature, getting home late, and a reference cost for the room. It gives no street address for your accommodation; use the one on your booking.

Prices are in S$ with INR at S$1 = ₹74.10.

## The area

- Hillview is a quiet, residential pocket of condominiums and landed houses between the Bukit Timah ridge and the Bukit Timah Expressway, with about 20,600 residents in 2 square kilometres. It is part of the Bukit Batok planning area, and the name refers to its view of Bukit Timah Hill.
- From the early 1940s to the late 1980s it was an industrial area (Ford, Castrol, Union Carbide, Daimler-Benz and the Hume Pipe Company). The factories were replaced by housing; only the Former Ford Factory survives, about 1.1 km south.
- The quarries often mentioned in connection with the area are next door, at Singapore Quarry in Dairy Farm Nature Park and at Bukit Batok, not in Hillview itself.
- There is no nightlife district. The two small malls, The Rail Mall and HillV2, are the only clusters of activity, so evenings are quiet.
- Why it works as a base: the Downtown Line threads the city centre, so Botanic Gardens, Bugis, Bayfront, Telok Ayer and Chinatown are all single rides with no change, and nature trails start within 10 minutes' walk.

## Hillview station

- Address: 510 Upper Bukit Timah Road, Singapore 678211. Underground, on the Downtown Line only (no interchange). Operated by SBS Transit.
- Exit A is on the southbound side of Upper Bukit Timah Road, with a pick-up and drop-off point. Use it for The Rail Mall and Dairy Farm Road.
- Exit B is on the northbound side, with taxi stand H21 and a pick-up and drop-off point. Use it for HillV2, Hillview Road, the Hillview Community Club and the lift up to Hillview Bridge on the Rail Corridor.
- The two exits are about 80 m apart, on opposite sides of the road.
- The station is deep, because it doubles as a Civil Defence shelter: platforms are two levels below the concourse. Allow 3 to 4 minutes from the gates to the train. Lifts and escalators link both exits to the concourse.
- The concourse has a passenger service centre, ticketing kiosks, public toilets and an ATM.

### Nearest hawker centres

There is no hawker centre in Hillview. The nearest are two stops south at Beauty World (DT5), and the two centres there are a few minutes apart.

- Beauty World Food Centre, Level 4, 144 Upper Bukit Timah Road: Hong Wen Mutton Soup (#04-20, in the 2026 Michelin Guide, mixed mutton soup about S$10 (₹741)), Top 1 Home Made Noodle (#04-44, handmade noodles since 1989, dry ban mian about S$5.50 (₹408)), Blanco Court Fresh Fish Soup (#04-21, since 1978) and 28 Economical Rice (#04-66, S$3.50 to S$6 (₹259 to ₹445)). Recommended because it puts a full hawker centre two stops from your door.
- Bukit Timah Interim Hawker Centre and Market, 2A Jalan Seh Chuan: the temporary home of the Bukit Timah centre until the permanent one reopens around 2029. Stalls named in reviews include Sin Chew Satay Bee Hoon, He Zhong Carrot Cake, Xie Kee Hokkien Mee, Seng Heng Hainanese Boneless Chicken Rice, Yong Seng Satay, Terry Katong Laksa, a Nyonya chendol stall described as Michelin-recommended, and Living Wholesome Vegetarian Food.
- Senja Hawker Centre, 2 Senja Close, north of Hillview via Bukit Panjang and the LRT: 28 stalls, opened December 2022, including Heng Gi Goose and Duck Rice and Amoy Street Lor Mee. Its opening hours conflict between sources.

## Nature and heritage on the doorstep

These are in the plan's spare list for any early morning before the heat. Park hours make early starts the natural choice: most parks open at 7am.

- Dairy Farm Nature Park: starts about 600 to 750 m east of the station. Open 7am to 7pm. The Wallace Education Centre opens 8:30am to 5pm and is closed on Mondays. Recommended as the single best outing from Hillview, because it starts within 10 minutes' walk and has a clear destination in the quarry deck.
- Singapore Quarry, inside Dairy Farm Nature Park: about 900 m away. Open 8am to 6:30pm (NParks; older guides give shorter hours).
- Rail Corridor: join it by lift from Exit B onto Hillview Bridge, whose deck has embedded railway tracks and a lookout over Bukit Timah Nature Reserve and Dairy Farm. Walk south to the restored Bukit Timah Railway Station and its 1932 Story cafe, then return by MRT from King Albert Park (DT6). The corridor is not lit at night, so leave by nightfall. Recommended because the walk starts at your station gate.
- Bukit Timah Nature Reserve: Singapore's highest hill (163 m), 1.5 to 1.9 km away; nearest MRT Beauty World. Open 7am to 7pm. Summit demolition works run from July 2026 to March 2028; only a non-official source says the summit stays open, so check the NParks notice.
- Zhenghua Nature Park and Bukit Batok Nature Park: open 24 hours, about 1.4 km away, the only doorstep options outside 7am to 7pm.
- Little Guilin (Bukit Batok Town Park): a flooded granite quarry, about 1.6 km west; a bus is easier than the walk.
- Chestnut Nature Park: 81 hectares of hiking and mountain biking trails, the weakest fit; part of the Blue Quail Trail is closed for slope repair until the fourth quarter of 2026.
- Former Ford Factory, 351 Upper Bukit Timah Road: where the British surrender of Singapore was signed on 15 February 1942. About 1.1 km south. Open Tuesday to Sunday 9am to 5:30pm, closed Mondays; admission was not found.
- Trail closures to check before you go: Hindhede Nature Park trails from the car park (reported closed 1 September to 31 December 2026 by a non-official source only), and "some trails" at Bukit Batok Nature Park (no dates given).

Wear closed shoes with grip on the trails, carry water, and use repellent.

## Getting home late

- Last trains at Hillview: the last train towards Bukit Panjang (your homeward direction) reaches Hillview at 12:45am. The last departures from city stations were not found, so aim to board in the city by about midnight and check MyTransport.SG on the day.
- The last train from Hillview into the city leaves at 11:38pm, so a late start into town is limited.
- There are no night buses. After the last train, take a taxi or Grab.
- Taxi costs from the city centre, about 20 km (estimates): about S$18 (₹1,334) before surcharges, about S$23 (₹1,704) from 5pm to 11:59pm, and about S$27 (₹2,001) after midnight, when the 50 percent surcharge applies. Getting home before midnight saves money.
- Drop-off points: Hillview MRT Exit B has taxi stand H21 and a pick-up point, and Exit A has a pick-up point. The Rail Mall has its own drop-off and parking.
- After dark, use the roads, not the Rail Corridor, which is unlit.
- For the 4:30am taxi to the airport on 9 November, book a ComfortDelGro taxi the evening before and keep Grab as a backup.
