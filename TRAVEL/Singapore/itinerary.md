# Itinerary: Singapore, Monday 2 to Monday 9 November 2026

This is the agreed plan from `itinerary-current.json`, written out day by day. The order of stops, the "also" options and the spare list follow that plan. Where the research notes disagree with the plan, or a fact is uncertain, the text says so.

How to read it:

- Prices are for one person unless marked "for two". S$ figures carry INR equivalents at S$1 = ₹74.10, the mid-market rate on 22 October 2026 used in the budget notes. Banks and forex cards will give a slightly worse rate.
- Durations are estimates unless a source is named.
- Anything marked "estimate" is a figure built from cited prices or from judgement, not a quoted price.
- Hours and prices from older reviews are dated where the notes give a date. Check them on the day.
- The map itinerary (`singapore-2026-map.html`) is now editable: you can tick places done, delete them and drag them between days. Those changes are not reflected in these files, which follow `itinerary-current.json` as it stood on 25 October 2026.

## Day 1: Monday 2 November. Land, Jewel, then Hillview

Area: Changi Airport and Jewel. Getting there: The inbound flight lands in the evening. Jewel is linked to the terminals; the taxi to Hillview afterwards takes about 35 minutes.

Weather, plan note: You land in the evening, inside the usual evening storm window. Storms can hold arrivals for a short while; the taxi rank is under cover.

Weather, early forecast (low confidence, recheck around 31 Oct): high 30.4°C, low 22.4°C, feels like 33.8°C. Rain chance: morning 53%, afternoon 99%, evening 100%. UV index 8.5. Typical for 2 November: high 30.0°C, low 24.0°C, 16.7 mm of rain, wet in 9 of 10 years.

### Morning and afternoon

In the air on the inbound flight from Mumbai. Before you board, check that both SG Arrival Cards were submitted (the window opens on Saturday 31 October, Singapore time) and that the e-Pass emails have arrived.

### Evening

#### Arrival at Changi

- What: immigration, bags and a local SIM or eSIM.
- Why: the automated lanes need no enrolment for first-time visitors with an ICAO-compliant passport, so clearance should be quick.
- Duration: the plan aims for you to be out with your bags by about 20:45.
- Cost: none. A tourist eSIM or SIM is about S$12 (₹889) each (Singtel 100 GB for 14 days, or M1 100 GB for 15 days).
- Tips: The inbound flight most likely uses Terminal 2, but flight trackers disagree (T1, T2 and T3 have all been reported), so confirm in the SIA app on the day. Do not cover your face at the automated lanes.

#### Jewel Changi Airport

- What: the glass-domed complex linked to the terminals by a link bridge, about a 10-minute walk, with the Rain Vortex indoor waterfall at its centre.
- Why: it is on your way out, it is indoors during the likely evening storm, and the first night needs nothing more ambitious. The Rain Vortex light show runs at 8pm and 9pm on Mondays and the waterfall runs until 10pm, so the 9pm show is catchable if the bags come quickly.
- Duration: about an hour (estimate).
- Cost: entry is free. The 24-hour Smarte Carte counter stores bags for about S$11 to S$16 (₹815 to ₹1,186) a bag. Supper for two is about S$25 to S$30 (₹1,853 to ₹2,223 estimate).
- Supper options: Devil Chicken in Basement 2 (#B2-258) closes at 9:30pm, so go there first. Tokyo Milk Cheese Factory (#B2-265, listed 10am to 10pm) is next to it, and the Food Republic food court runs until 11pm if you are late. Shake Shack and a Birds of Paradise gelato boutique, both on your wish list, are also in Jewel; their hours were not checked.
- Tips: leave the big bags with Smarte Carte before you look around, then collect them on the way out.

#### Taxi to Hillview

- What: a taxi or ride-hail to your accommodation near Hillview MRT, Downtown Line DT3.
- Why: the MRT route needs a change at Expo and takes well over an hour with luggage. A taxi is about 35 minutes.
- Duration: about 35 minutes.
- Cost: a metered taxi is about S$55 to S$75 (₹4,076 to ₹5,558 estimate), including the 25 percent evening surcharge and the S$8 airport surcharge that apply from 5pm to 11:59pm. The Changi Ground Transport Concierge offers a fixed S$70 (₹5,187) transfer in a four-seater, bookable in the Changi App or at the counters in the arrival halls. Grab shows a fixed price before you book but can surge in rain.
- Tips: leave before midnight. From 12am to 5:59am a 50 percent surcharge applies to the meter. The Concierge counters are in the arrival halls (Terminal 2: Level 1, Door 6; Terminal 3: Level 1, next to the information counter); the notes do not cover a taxi stand at Jewel itself. If you are hungry when you reach Hillview, The Rail Mall's Springleaf Prata Place and Cold Storage run 24 hours (see the spare list).

## Day 2: Tuesday 3 November. Tiong Bahru, Chinatown and the Esplanade

Area: Tiong Bahru, Chinatown, Marina Bay. Getting there: Downtown Line to Chinatown, about 35 minutes. For Tiong Bahru, change at Chinatown to the North East Line for Outram Park. The notes also give the Downtown Line to Stevens and the Thomson-East Coast Line to Havelock, at the north edge of the estate.

Weather, plan note: Mornings are usually the driest part of the day, which suits Tiong Bahru and Maxwell. Storms tend to build in the late afternoon, when you are resting. The Esplanade Concourse is indoors; if Satay Street is wet, eat inside the Lau Pa Sat pavilion.

Weather, early forecast (low confidence, recheck around 31 Oct): high 31.0°C, low 22.2°C, feels like 34.3°C. Rain chance: morning 63%, afternoon 100%, evening 100%. UV index 8.9. Typical for 3 November: high 29.5°C, low 24.0°C, 11.4 mm of rain, wet in 10 of 10 years.

### Morning

#### Tiong Bahru Market

- What: a hawker centre above a wet market at 30 Seng Poh Road. Your stall is Tiong Bahru Hainanese Boneless Chicken Rice, #02-82.
- Why: it is the Tiong Bahru chicken rice on your wish list, a 2026 Michelin Bib Gourmand, and open today (its rest day is Monday).
- Duration: about 45 minutes.
- Cost: an older price is about S$3 a plate; 2026 heartland chicken rice runs S$4.50 to S$6 (₹333 to ₹445). Breakfast for two is about S$14 (₹1,037 budget notes estimate).
- Tips: the stall opens at 10am and closes once the chickens sell out, so arrive around 10am. Reviews are mixed between the white and the roast chicken; the roast is popular. Jian Bo Tiong Bahru Shui Kueh is also on the 2026 Bib Gourmand list if you arrive earlier and want breakfast first.

#### Tiong Bahru Estate

- What: 1930s Art Deco and Streamline Moderne flats built by the Singapore Improvement Trust from 1936 and conserved in 2003.
- Why: it is the most compact example of the heritage architecture on your list, and it is a short walk from the market.
- Duration: about 45 minutes.
- Cost: free.
- Tips: the best-preserved blocks are along Tiong Poh Road, Yong Siak Street and Eng Hoon Street. Block 81 is said to resemble an airship and Block 82 an ocean liner. Woods in the Books, at 3 Yong Siak Street, lists Tuesday to Saturday 11am to 8pm.

#### Amoy Street Food Centre

- What: a hawker centre at 7 Maxwell Road. From Tiong Bahru, take the Thomson-East Coast Line from Havelock two stops to Maxwell and walk about five minutes.
- Why: J2 Famous Crispy Curry Puff (#01-21) is the curry puff on your wish list and a 2026 Bib Gourmand. It trades Monday to Friday only and its puffs sell out early, so it comes before lunch.
- Duration: about 20 minutes.
- Cost: S$1.40 to S$2 (₹104 to ₹148) a puff.
- Tips: J2 opens 8am to 3pm. Fillings include curry potato, sardine, black pepper chicken and yam.

#### Maxwell Food Centre

- What: a central hawker centre at 1 Kadayanallur Street, five minutes from Amoy Street and a short walk from Chinatown station.
- Why: it holds four of your named dishes in one place, plus Tian Tian chicken rice.
- What to order: Tian Tian Hainanese Chicken Rice (#01-10/11, 2026 Bib Gourmand; Michelin suggests half a chicken to share). Rojak, Popiah & Cockle (#01-56; 11:30am to 8:30pm, closed Wednesday; popiah made to order; cockles only on selected days). Maxwell Fuzhou Oyster Cake (#01-05; 9am to 8pm, closed Sunday; S$2 to S$2.50 (₹148 to ₹185) a cake, S$1 more for extra oysters). Old Nyonya laksa (#01-04; S$5 (₹371) or S$7 (₹519)). Fu Shun roast meats (#01-71; closed Sunday; queues up to 30 minutes).
- Duration: about an hour.
- Cost: lunch for two about S$18 to S$30 (₹1,334 to ₹2,223 estimate).
- Tips: the plan uses Tian Tian today because Ah Tai (#01-07), the usual shorter-queue alternative, is closed on Tuesdays. Tian Tian's queue runs about 10 minutes at opening or mid-afternoon and 45 to 90 minutes from 11:30am to 1:30pm, so arrive before 11:30am, or have one of you queue for chicken rice while the other collects the rest. Tian Tian's 2026 price and unit number were not confirmed.

### Afternoon

#### Ann Siang Road

- What: a short conservation street of shophouses above Telok Ayer, with Club Street beside it.
- Why: Kokuyo's first Singapore stationery shop is here, inside its office and showroom at 31 and 33 Ann Siang Road (opened 28 November 2025), with Campus notebooks and Harinacs staplers.
- Duration: about 30 minutes.
- Cost: free to browse.
- Tips: Kokuyo's opening hours were not found, so check before you go. The shop is described as small, with a restock reported in early October 2026.

#### Buddha Tooth Relic Temple

- What: a Buddhist temple and museum at 288 South Bridge Road.
- Why: it is on your walking route between Maxwell and the Hindu temple, and it is free to enter.
- Duration: about 45 minutes.
- Cost: free; donations welcome.
- Tips: official hours are 9am to 5pm (other guides differ). Shoulders and knees must be covered; shorts, mini-skirts and off-shoulder tops are not allowed, and sarongs are available at the entrance. No photography in the fourth-floor relic chamber.

#### Sri Mariamman Temple

- What: Singapore's oldest Hindu temple, at 244 South Bridge Road, beside Masjid Jamae (Chulia) at number 218.
- Why: it is a few minutes from the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, and a Tuesday visit avoids the Deepavali crowds of Sunday 8 November.
- Duration: about 30 minutes.
- Cost: free.
- Tips: remove your shoes and cover shoulders and knees. Hours and any camera fee were not verified.

#### Chinatown Complex Food Centre

- What: a large hawker centre at 335 Smith Street with more than 260 stalls.
- Why: three wish-list items are here: Ann Chin Handmade Popiah (#02-112), Old Amoy Chendol (#02-008) and 168 CMY Satay (#02-168).
- What to order: popiah rolled to order (an older price is S$1.60 a roll); traditional chendol with gula melaka and coconut milk (S$2.80 (₹207), July 2025); satay at about S$0.60 a stick with a minimum of ten (2021 price, expect more), served with a peanut sauce with pineapple puree. CMY's satay is pork, chicken and mutton.
- Duration: about 45 minutes.
- Cost: snacks for two about S$12 to S$20 (₹889 to ₹1,482 estimate).
- Tips: CMY closes at 7pm and is closed on Mondays. Mid-afternoon is quieter than lunch. Return trays to the racks. Your three bakeries are close by: Pretty Good Muffins in Hong Lim Complex (until 3:30pm, so go there first if you want it), Rise Bakehouse at 273 South Bridge Road and Dome Bakery at 227 South Bridge Road. Details are in the Day 2 "also" list.

Rest through the hottest hours, then head for Marina Bay.

#### Apple Marina Bay Sands

- What: the Apple store on the water at Marina Bay Sands (2 Bayfront Avenue, B2-06), the iPhone stop.
- Why: the travellers want to buy the iPhone early in the trip. This store sits between Chinatown and the Esplanade, three stops on the Downtown Line from Chinatown to Bayfront with no change.
- Duration: about an hour (estimate).
- Cost: the iPhone 18 Pro 256 GB is S$1,899 (₹1,40,716) including GST. It is not in the trip budget.
- Tips: open 10am to 10pm in late October; November hours were not yet published. The buyer brings the original passport and a card in the same name. Before paying, ask the Specialist to confirm that an eTRS (tourist refund) transaction will be issued, pay in SGD, and check that the receipt shows the serial number and the eTRS ticket. Keep the box sealed until the GST refund at Changi on 9 November, and declare the phone at Mumbai (allowance ₹75,000 per person). The worked example in `budget_summary.md` saves about ₹11,000 on a 256 GB Pro and is roughly break-even at 1 TB and above; `safety_and_tips.md` has the steps. Apple Orchard Road is the backup on Day 4.

### Evening

#### Esplanade Theatres on the Bay

- What: a free Soundtracks performance, "As Heard on TV!", on the Esplanade Concourse.
- Why: it is the Marina Bay free concert on your wish list. The free Soundtracks shows in the window run only from 2 to 5 November.
- Duration: 30 minutes a show.
- Cost: free; the event page states no registration requirement.
- Tips: shows at 6:45pm and 7:45pm. Walk round the bay from Marina Bay Sands. The Concourse is indoors.

#### Lau Pa Sat (Satay Street)

- What: a Victorian market hall at 18 Raffles Quay. In the evening, Boon Tat Street beside it closes to traffic and satay stalls set up from about 7pm.
- Why: satay under the office towers is the Lau Pa Sat item on your wish list, and the street is the draw.
- Duration: about an hour.
- Cost: Best Satay (stalls 7 and 8) Set A is S$31.20 (₹2,312) for 10 chicken, 10 mutton or beef and 6 prawn sticks (May 2026). Dinner with drinks for two is about S$38 (₹2,816 budget notes estimate).
- Tips: stalls 7 and 8 have the longest queues, about 30 minutes with a buzzer. One reviewer called the satay average and the atmosphere the reason to go. From the Esplanade, take the Downtown Line two stops from Promenade to Downtown, or walk. The Downtown Line runs home direct from Downtown or Telok Ayer.

## Day 3: Wednesday 4 November. Botanic Gardens to the Southern Ridges

Area: Botanic Gardens, NUS, Southern Ridges, Alexandra. Getting there: Downtown Line to Botanic Gardens (six stops, no change), then five stops on the Circle Line to Kent Ridge.

Weather, plan note: The Southern Ridges are the most exposed walk of the week. If thunder starts, shelter at HortPark or go straight down to Keng Eng Kee; Henderson Waves stays lit until 2am, so you can still see it after the rain.

Weather, early forecast (low confidence, recheck around 31 Oct): high 30.8°C, low 23.4°C, feels like 35.0°C. Rain chance: morning 50%, afternoon 92%, evening 95%. UV index 8.9. Typical for 4 November: high 29.7°C, low 23.9°C, 9.2 mm of rain, wet in 10 of 10 years.

### Morning

#### Singapore Botanic Gardens

- What: a UNESCO World Heritage garden, open 5am to midnight.
- Why: it is on your list, it is direct from Hillview, and it is the best cool-morning walk of the trip.
- Duration: three to four hours with the Orchid Garden.
- Cost: free.
- Tips: arrive from 7am while it is cool. Enter at Bukit Timah Gate beside Botanic Gardens MRT, then walk south through the Learning Forest to the Orchid Garden, Swan Lake, the Bandstand and the Heritage Museum. Tapping in at Hillview before 7:45am on a weekday takes 50 cents off the fare.

#### National Orchid Garden

- What: the one ticketed section of the Botanic Gardens.
- Why: it is the main collection in the Gardens and opens early, before the heat.
- Duration: about an hour.
- Cost: S$15 (₹1,112) each.
- Tips: open 8:30am to 7pm, last entry 6pm. Klook charges the same price as the gate, so there is no reason to prebook.

#### NUS Frontier canteen

- What: a university canteen at the Faculty of Science, about 590 m from Kent Ridge MRT. This is the "hospital canteen" wish-list item, now the NUS cafeterias.
- Why: it is a cheap student lunch on the way to the Southern Ridges.
- What to order: Chef's Wok (halal; hor fun or duck noodles S$2.30 (₹170), lemon chicken rice S$2.60 (₹193)) or Uncle Penyet (ayam penyet set S$5.90 (₹437)). Prices are from October 2025.
- Duration: about 45 minutes.
- Cost: lunch for two about S$10 to S$15 (₹741 to ₹1,112 estimate).
- Tips: early November is late teaching term, so go before noon or after 1:30pm. Carry a card as well as cash; payment methods and public access were not confirmed. The Deck (Arts), about 1.4 km from the MRT, is the second choice (weekdays 8am to 6pm; yong tau foo or laksa S$3.50 (₹259)).

### Afternoon

Rest through the hottest hours, then start the walk in the late afternoon.

#### Kent Ridge Park

- What: the western start of the Southern Ridges, about 1 km from the Frontier canteen.
- Why: starting at the western end means you reach Henderson Waves at about the time its lights come on.
- Duration: about 30 minutes.
- Cost: free.
- Tips: the 280 m Canopy Walk runs to Reflections at Bukit Chandu (open Tuesday to Sunday 9:30am to 5pm). Walk or take a short taxi from NUS.

#### HortPark

- What: themed gardens midway along the Southern Ridges, 1.4 km from Kent Ridge Park.
- Why: it is the shelter point on the walk if a storm comes in.
- Duration: about 30 minutes, plus the walk.
- Cost: free.
- Tips: part of the Forest Walk east of Alexandra Arch (the eastern elevated walkway, the Earth Trail and the northern loop of Telok Blangah Green) closed for slope repairs with a target of 31 July 2026, and no reopening notice was found. Check NParks before you set out; if it is still shut, walk along Alexandra Road or take a short taxi to Henderson Waves.

### Evening

#### Henderson Waves

- What: a 274 m wave-shaped pedestrian bridge, 36 m up, the tallest pedestrian bridge in Singapore. It is 1.6 km from HortPark.
- Why: it is on your "check out and decide" list, and the LED lighting comes on at 7pm.
- Duration: about 30 minutes.
- Cost: free.
- Tips: lit 7pm to 2am daily. Avoid walking the ridge between 11am and 3pm. If there is lightning, wait at HortPark; the bridge stays lit late.

#### Keng Eng Kee Seafood

- What: a zi char (Chinese cooked food) restaurant at #01-136, 124 Bukit Merah Lane 1, Alexandra Village. This is your crab dinner.
- Why: chilli crab is on your list, and the notes describe KEK as a strong mid-range choice with a better atmosphere than the tourist-strip crab houses.
- What to order: one crab of about 1 kg with mantou to mop up the sauce, plus coffee pork ribs or moonlight hor fun (dish names from general coverage; no 2026 menu was found).
- Duration: about 1.5 hours.
- Cost: chilli crab is market-priced at about S$100 (₹7,410) a kilo (Time Out). Dinner for two with sides is about S$150 to S$220 (₹11,115 to ₹16,302 estimate).
- Tips: book ahead (+65 6272 1038); crab often sells out for walk-ins. Open daily 11am to 2pm and 5pm to 10pm. Ask for the crab's weight and the total price before it is cooked. Lina Confectionery two doors along (#01-138) closes at 8pm on weekdays, so the plan has you buy the kueh first and then eat. That means leaving Henderson Waves by about 7:20pm; the bridge stays lit until 2am if you want to go back. Lina sells bika ambon (about S$1.50 (₹111)), kueh kosui and onde-onde (about S$2.60 (₹193)) and ang ku kueh (about S$1.20 (₹89)), prices from about 2023. Take a short taxi down from the ridge; Queenstown MRT is about 1 km from KEK.

### Also on Day 3

- Mount Faber Park: the eastern start of the Southern Ridges, with the second official Merlion statue near the start of the walk.
- Singapore Indoor Stadium: LANY plays the "Soft World Tour" at 8pm. One listing gives tickets from S$138 (₹10,226); resale starts around S$77 (₹5,706), but the venue warns that resold tickets will not be honoured. Getting home needs a Circle Line to Downtown Line change after about 10:30pm.

## Day 4: Thursday 5 November. Wetlands, wildlife and Orchard by night

Area: Sungei Buloh, Mandai, Orchard Road. Getting there: bus 925 from Kranji to Sungei Buloh, the Mandai shuttle from Khatib to the Zoo, then the North South Line from Khatib to Orchard.

Weather, plan note: Sungei Buloh at 7am is the coolest and usually driest slot of the day. The Zoo has covered walkways and Orchard is indoors in the evening, so this day copes well with a late storm.

Weather, early forecast (low confidence, recheck around 31 Oct): high 30.3°C, low 23.0°C, feels like 33.8°C. Rain chance: morning 54%, afternoon 99%, evening 100%. UV index 8.9. Typical for 5 November: high 29.6°C, low 23.9°C, 9.3 mm of rain, wet in 8 of 10 years.

### Morning

#### Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve

- What: mangroves and mudflats in the north-west, with the Coastal Trail, the Migratory Bird Trail and the Mangrove Boardwalk.
- Why: it is on your list, and the migratory season runs from August to April, so early November is in season.
- Duration: about two hours.
- Cost: historically free; no 2026 entry policy was found.
- Tips: open 7am to 7pm, last entry 6:30pm. The Visitor Centre is at 60 Kranji Way. Bus 925 runs from Kranji MRT Monday to Saturday. There is no guided walk on a Thursday. Keep a distance from estuarine crocodiles and do not enter flooded or closed areas. Use repellent. To be there at 7am, a taxi or Grab is the practical option, about S$22 (₹1,630 budget notes estimate): the first Downtown Line train towards Bukit Panjang leaves Hillview at 6:13am, and the public transport route via Choa Chu Kang and Kranji then takes a while.

#### Singapore Zoo

- What: the open-concept zoo at Mandai.
- Why: it is on your list, and Thursday is a regular-price weekday.
- Duration: three to four hours (the plan; the sites notes suggest four to five).
- Cost: S$49 (₹3,631) each for tourists on a regular day. Lunch and snacks for two about S$60 (₹4,446 budget notes estimate).
- Tips: from Sungei Buloh, take bus 925 back to Kranji, the North South Line to Khatib (seven stops: Marsiling, Woodlands, Admiralty, Sembawang, Canberra, Yishun, Khatib; the plan says five), then the Mandai Khatib Shuttle (M2) from Exit A, every 15 minutes from 8:30am, S$2.50 (₹185). The shuttle is not covered by the Singapore Tourist Pass. The budget notes priced a Grab between the two parks at about S$18 (₹1,334) instead, which saves about an hour of buses in the heat. Zoo hours and feeding times were not retrieved; check the Mandai website and buy tickets online.

### Afternoon

Finish at the Zoo, then take the shuttle back to Khatib. The North South Line runs straight to Orchard, eight stops.

### Evening

#### Takashimaya and Ngee Ann City

- What: a department store and mall at 391 Orchard Road, with a food hall in Basement 2 and Books Kinokuniya in the building.
- Why: it is the "Takashimaya (?)" on your wish list, and it is indoors for the evening storm window.
- Duration: about an hour.

## Seasonal notes for 2 to 9 November 2026

- Season: early November is the second inter-monsoon period, not the Northeast Monsoon, which starts in December. Expect hot, humid mornings and afternoon or early-evening thunderstorms that can be heavy but short. The long-run November average (1991 to 2020, Changi) is 252.3 mm of rain over about 19 rain days, highs of 31.2°C, lows of 24.6°C and humidity near 85 percent. November has the most lightning days of the year (22).
- This year: MSS classifies strong El Niño conditions, forecast to peak from October to December 2026, with a positive Indian Ocean Dipole through November. Both point to drier and hotter than average. Early October highs were forecast at 34°C to 35°C.
- Haze: 2026 is a haze year. The 24-hour PSI reached 181 to 191 in central Singapore on 9 October, and a 16 October report cited 225. Check the NEA haze page or the myENV app each morning from 30 October. Pack N95 masks and keep an indoor fallback for each day: the National Gallery on Day 6, Takashimaya and the malls on Day 4, Parkway Parade on Day 5, the Gardens' domes, and Jewel.
- Lightning: outdoor facilities may suspend operations during lightning alerts; Garden Rhapsody can pause.
- Dengue: cases are low by recent standards, but three red-alert clusters were active in mid-October (Lentor Gardens, Bishan Street 12 and the Ho Ching Road area). Use repellent at Sungei Buloh, the Zoo, the trails and the kampong.
- Deepavali: Sunday 8 November, with Monday 9 November as the public holiday in lieu. You ruled out Little India, so this is for information: the Serangoon Road light-up runs nightly from 7pm to midnight, and the Campbell Lane Festival Village runs until 8 November. Many Indian-run stalls and shops shorten their hours on 8 and 9 November.
- Long weekend: Saturday 7 to Monday 9 November is one of six long weekends in 2026. Book timed entry and dinner tables for these days ahead. Changi is busy on Friday 6 and Saturday 7 November for outbound residents and on Monday 9 November for returns.
- School term: Singapore schools do not break until 22 November, so local family crowds are lighter than in the holidays. At NUS, 2 to 6 November is late teaching term, so canteens are crowded at lunch.
- Gardens by the Bay maintenance closures: Floral Fantasy on 3 November, the OCBC Skyway on 4 November and the Supertree Observatory on 5 November. The Flower Dome and Cloud Forest have no closures from 2 to 9 November.
- Events just outside the window: the Singapore Writers Festival runs 13 to 22 November and My Chemical Romance plays on 10 and 11 November, both after you leave. The Orchard Road Christmas light-up has no confirmed 2026 date; the 2025 edition started on 8 November, so it could fall on 7 or 14 November. The Deepavali Istana Open House date is unconfirmed; blogs project 1 November, the day before you land.
- Ticketed events in the window not in the plan: "From Solo to Dialogue", a piano recital at the Esplanade Concert Hall, Saturday 7 November at 7:30pm (price not found); "Pán" by T.H.E Dance Company at the Chinese Cultural Centre on 6 and 7 November (time and price not listed); "How To Be A Kid" at Gateway Theatre, 5 to 15 November, about S$45 (₹3,335).
