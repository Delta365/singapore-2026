# Transportation

You will travel mostly by MRT and bus, paying by tapping a contactless bank card, with taxis for the two airport runs and the odd late or early ride. You do not drive, so there is no driving or parking section.

Prices are in S$ with INR at S$1 = ₹74.10. Taxi and journey-time figures are estimates unless a source is given.

## Arrival: Monday 2 November

- Your flight from Mumbai lands in the evening. The terminal is most likely Terminal 2, but trackers disagree (T1, T2 and T3 have all been reported), so check the SIA app on the day.
- Immigration: submit the SG Arrival Card for each of you from Saturday 31 October (Singapore time), through the free ICA e-Service or the MyICA app. Use the automated lanes; first-time visitors need no enrolment. The e-Pass arrives by email.
- The plan: out with bags by about 20:45, walk the link bridge to Jewel (about 10 minutes), supper there, then a taxi to Hillview, leaving before midnight.

## Paying for the MRT and buses

### Tap a contactless card

- Each of you taps your own contactless Visa, Mastercard or American Express card, or the same card in Apple Pay or Google Pay, at the MRT gates and on the bus readers. There is nothing to buy and no account to open.
- A foreign card is charged a S$0.60 Foreign Bank Card Admin Fee for each day it is used, not each journey. Over the trip that is about S$4.80 (₹356) each. Your bank may add its own foreign transaction fee, so a zero-markup card is worth using.
- Use the same card or the same phone to tap in and out. Mixing a plastic card and a phone wallet can lead to double charges, even for the same card. Two people cannot share one card.
- Maestro, Cirrus and NETSPay cards are not accepted.
- Charges reach your bank in batches. Mastercard fares accumulate for up to five days or until S$15; Visa and American Express accumulate daily. The SimplyGo app shows each journey if you want to check; the SimplyGo web portal closed on 8 January 2026.
- If Google Pay gives trouble at the gates, remove and re-add the card; the SimplyGo FAQ mentions an expired certificate issue.
- Check before you leave India that both cards are enabled for international contactless payments.

### Other options and why they are worse here

- EZ-Link card: S$10 (₹741), of which S$5 is a non-refundable card cost. Foreign cards cannot top it up at the kiosks, so top-ups mean cash or a staffed ticket office.
- Singapore Tourist Pass: unlimited rides for 1 day S$17 (₹1,260), 2 days S$24 (₹1,778), 3 days S$29 (₹2,149), 4 days S$37 (₹2,742) or 5 days S$45 (₹3,335). There is no 8-day pass, and covering the trip would cost about S$74 (₹5,483) each. Paying as you go at three or four journeys a day costs less. Sources disagree on whether there is a refundable S$10 deposit, so assume none. The pass does not cover the M2 Mandai Khatib shuttle.
- NETS FlashPay: a third-party guide says it stopped working on public transport from 1 June 2024. Do not rely on an old card.

## Buses

- Board at the front and tap your card on the reader; tap again on the reader by the exit door when you get off. This is standard practice, though the notes did not find an official page stating it or the penalty for forgetting to tap out.
- Cash fares need the exact amount, give no change and cost 60 to 80 cents more than card fares.
- Since 23 September 2026, eating or drinking on a bus, including plain water, can be fined up to S$500, as can feet on seats and loud audio. Soiling a bus or interchange can be fined up to S$5,000. Drivers will usually ask you to stop first.
- Stops are not reliably announced, so use the alight reminder in a bus app or follow your position on a map.
- Buses at Hillview station: stop 43069 at Exit A serves 67, 75, 170, 178, 184, 961, 961M and 973; stop 43061 at Exit B serves 67, 75, 170, 176, 178, 184, 961, 961M, 963 and 970. Buses 67, 75, 170, 178 and 184 run down Upper Bukit Timah Road if the Downtown Line is disrupted, and bridging buses run from the same stops.
- Buses used in the plan: 925 from Kranji MRT to Sungei Buloh (Monday to Saturday; 925M on Sundays and public holidays); the M2 Mandai Khatib Shuttle from Khatib Exit A to the Zoo (every 15 minutes from 8:30am, S$2.50 (₹185)); 36, 48 or 196 to Marine Parade Road for East Coast Park on a weekday; bus 2 from Upper Changi or Tanah Merah to Changi Village on a swap day.

## Rules on trains and buses

- No eating or drinking on trains, buses, stations or interchanges, including water. The MRT fine is up to S$500 (travel guides; the legal text was not retrieved); the bus fine is up to S$500 under the 2026 regulations.
- No smoking: the statutory maximum under the Rapid Transit Systems Act is S$1,000.
- No durians on the MRT.
- Hillview station is deep (it doubles as a Civil Defence shelter). Lifts and escalators link the exits to the concourse; Exit A's lift is confirmed, and Exit B has a lift to Hillview Bridge. Allow 3 to 4 minutes from the gates to the platform.
- No MRT luggage size limit was found.
